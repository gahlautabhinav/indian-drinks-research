## SRC-347 — The Hindu report on the GI application process

**Citation:** Prabalika M. Borah, “Assam’s Dimasa people’s Judima rice wine finally gets a GI tag,” *The Hindu*, September 2021 (republished by Inkl; original date not exposed in the accessible reproduction).  
**Accessible reproduction:** https://www.inkl.com/news/assam-s-dimasa-people-s-judima-gets-gi-tag

The report quotes Uttam Bathari, managing trustee of YADEM, explaining:
- YADEM initiated the GI proposal in 2018.
- Following advice that a women’s group should apply because Judima is traditionally made by women, a group called Dimajik Hosom (described as a Dimasa women’s organisation) was formed.
- The 2019 application was made “along with YADEM.”
- Dibarai village council, Dibarai Mahila Samiti and Assam Agricultural University supported the GI proposal.

This explains why official and institutional records can display Dimajik Hosom as applicant while other government/secondary records identify YADEM as proprietor, representative or facilitator. It is not sufficient to conclude a transfer or legal equivalence of entities; rather, the sources describe a women’s collective and an assisting organisation participating in the application process.

The same report attributes to Bathari a description of three rice categories (red/white bora, non-bora and Bairing) and describes Thembra and Dhemera. These are attributed interview statements, not a substitute for the registered specification or botanical voucher evidence. Do not infer that every recipe uses all rice categories or that “Dhemera” is taxonomically resolved by this story.

## SRC-348 — Pangging et al. (2023), peer-reviewed GI applicant/facilitator table

**Citation:** G. Pangging, M. Sharma, C. L. Sharma, N. Rai and J. Gogoi (2023), “Status of Geographical Indications in the Northeast Region of India,” *Journal of Intellectual Property Rights* 28, July 2023, pp. 304–311. DOI: 10.56042/jipr.v28i4.743.  
**Full text:** https://or.niscpr.res.in/index.php/JIPR/article/download/743/794/8016

Table 1 (printed pp. 306–307) records Judima, application 643, in the Class 33 category “Alcoholic Beverages (Expect Beer) Made of Rice.” It lists:
- Applicant: DIMAJIK HOSOM, Dibarai village, Haflong, Dima Hasao, Assam.
- Facilitated by: (1) Youth Association for Development & Empowerment (YADEM); (2) Assam Agricultural University.

This independently corroborates the distinction between the applicant group and facilitating organisations. It supports interpreting the IP India “Applicant Name” field and APEDA “Registered Proprietor” display as differently framed institutional roles in the available sources, rather than alleging a proven inconsistency. The peer-reviewed table does not reproduce the underlying application form or establish whether YADEM has a separate legal status in the registered GI entry.

## SRC-115 / CTIL report cross-check — stakeholder-reported producer scale

The existing CTIL report by Annamma Samuel, *A Study on the Application and Coverage of Geographical Indications for Industrial/Manufactured Goods under India’s Free Trade Agreements*, was already registered as SRC-115. It reports qualitative discussions with Professor Uttam Bathari and the Dimajik Hosom women’s collective. It states approximately 200 women are involved in production and that formal licensing processes were still underway at the time of the report.

This figure is a stakeholder-reported approximate involvement estimate, not a verified count of:
- GI-authorised users;
- currently active producers;
- households or production units;
- producers listed in the 2019 GI application annexure;
- village-level producer locations.

The report separately records DIMAJIK HOSOM as the registered proprietor in its December 2024 manufactured-GI table and shows zero authorised users in that table's snapshot. Its 2024 registry snapshot must not be treated as current. The report is useful for a dated institutional snapshot and producer-scale lead, not as a live count.

Full text: https://ctil.org.in/docs/WCP_Study/_%20A%20STUDY%20ON%20THE%20APPLICATION%20AND%20COVERAGE%20OF%20GEOGRAPHICAL%20INDICATIONS%20FOR%20INDUSTRIALMANUFACTURED%20GOODS%20UNDER%20INDIA’S%20FREE%20TRADE%20AGREEMENTS%20(FTAs).pdf
