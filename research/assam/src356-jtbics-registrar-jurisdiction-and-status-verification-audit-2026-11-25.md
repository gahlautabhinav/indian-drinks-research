# SRC-356 — JTBICS official registry pathway, Haflong jurisdiction and status-verification limits

**Audit date:** 25 November 2026

## 1. Current Registrar of Cooperative Societies directory

The Assam Registrar of Cooperative Societies (RCS) public directory currently provides aggregate counts by cooperative type, not an individually searchable primary/district-level society register. Its page lists 369 industrial cooperatives in the state but does not enumerate them by name in the accessible HTML. It provides a downloadable list only for state-level societies; JTBICS is not located in that state-level list. This absence does not establish non-registration because the schedule classifies JTBICS under the Haflong subdivision and it is not known to be a state-level cooperative.

Official RCS list: https://rcs.assam.gov.in/information-services/detail/list-of-cooperative-societies

The Cooperation Department's parallel list says it maintains 12,333 societies, of which 7,734 are confirmed functional, but it also does not provide a name-level public list in the accessible page. Do not use either aggregate as JTBICS-specific status.

## 2. Relevant jurisdiction and official contacts listed by government

The RCS “Districts & Sub-divisions at a Glance” directory places Dima Hasao under Haflong and lists Haflong and Maibong subdivisions. The RCS/Cooperation Department officer directories show:
- District Deputy Registrar of Cooperative Societies, Haflong: Longbir Teron (RCS directory).
- Assistant Registrar of Cooperative Societies, Haflong: the official directory pages are not consistent in the named officer entry; one RCS page lists Dorothi Suantak Vaiphel at Haflong, while the Cooperation Department registration page lists Dilip Kr. Mazumdar at Haflong.
- Registration responsibility page states that district-level cooperatives are handled by the District Deputy Registrar; other primary cooperatives are handled by the Assistant Registrar.

Official pages:

- https://coop.assam.gov.in/about-us/detail/list-of-registrar

**Implication:** The correct office depends on JTBICS's registered classification and area of operation. The old schedule alone does not establish whether it is district-level or a primary cooperative. Do not assume the ARCS or DRCS is definitively the custodian until the society's classification/registration certificate is obtained. The differing ARCS names are a directory-maintenance inconsistency; neither name is treated as a confirmed point of contact for this research.

## 3. Registration procedure identifies the records that should exist

The current Assam RCS registration process states that a proposed cooperative registration file normally includes:
- application and feasibility report;
- proposed bye-laws (four copies);
- inaugural general meeting proceedings;
- list of promoters with age/domicile proof;
- registration fee challan;
- details of name, address, objects and area of operation.

The standard procedure also describes an initial application by at least ten adult promoters, feasibility assessment, and a registration process. These are generic statutory/procedural requirements, not proof that JTBICS's specific file survives or that all records are currently public.

This provides a precise records checklist for a future documentary verification:
1. registration certificate and registration number;
2. original application and registration date;
3. registered bye-laws and amendments;
4. inaugural general meeting minutes/promoter list;
5. area of operation and registered office;
6. current society status (functional, dormant, under liquidation/deregistration, or other recorded status);
7. latest annual returns, audit reports and election/board records;
8. member register and member-category data, subject to privacy and lawful access.

## 4. Re-reading SRC-354 schedule entry

The original PDF table explicitly labels its columns “Permission Accorded 2020–21” and “Permission Accorded 2021–22.” At serial 473 the cell values are “Do,” a continuation marker for the preceding “Accorded” entry; the same is true for many neighbouring societies. The schedule therefore records “permission accorded” in both years for the Judima society. The schedule does **not** identify the permission's subject in the extracted title or column headings, and no explanatory note has been recovered.

Do not infer this was an excise licence, alcohol manufacturing permission, registration status, annual operating licence, GI authorisation or quality approval. The exact permission type remains unknown. It is a historical entry, not a current status record.

## 5. Updated GI role cross-check: applicant vs registered proprietor

A live APEDA Assam GI page checked 25 November 2026 explicitly labels the Judima field as **Registered Proprietor** and names “Managing Trustee, Youth Association for Development & Empowerment (YADEM).” It gives application 643, certificate 376 and the 14 September 2021 certificate date. This is distinct from IP India's application details page, which labels DIMAJIK HOSOM as **Applicant Name**. Pangging et al. (2023) lists DIMAJIK HOSOM as applicant and YADEM/Assam Agricultural University as facilitators; The Hindu interview describes the application as made along with YADEM.

This supports a possible distinction between applicant group and registered proprietor, rather than an inherent contradiction. APEDA's 2022 catalogue also labels YADEM as Applicant Name, so the catalogue metadata is inconsistent with the current APEDA page's Registered Proprietor label. Preserve each source field and date exactly. The final legal position should be checked against the GI certificate and registered Statement of Case; do not claim an applicant amendment or transfer without those records.

## Archive decisions

1. Register this RCS directory and procedure cross-check as SRC-356.
2. Keep JTBICS's 2020–22 schedule record as historical “permission accorded,” subject unknown.
3. Do not mark JTBICS currently active or inactive.
4. Do not infer the responsible Haflong office until the society's legal classification is established.
5. Treat the registration file checklist as expected records under general process, not confirmed holdings.
6. Keep APEDA's live Registered Proprietor field (YADEM) distinct from IP India's Applicant Name field (DIMAJIK HOSOM); correct the previous audit language that called the role difference wholly unresolved.
7. No office, society, author or producer has been contacted; no RTI or record request has been made.
