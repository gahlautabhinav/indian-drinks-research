# SRC-139 and SRC-140 — Dibrugarh University bibliography cross-check

**Audit date:** 2026-11-04  
**Related gap:** ASM-GAP-062  
**Scope:** Verify the two 2011 Mising Epop/Saimod literature leads and distinguish bibliographic confirmation from full-text evidence.

## 1. Institutional source located

Dibrugarh University's *Volume IIB — Research Publication* PDF lists both works:

### SRC-139 — Saimod fermentation parameters
- Author: D. Kardong.
- Title: “Studies on the Changes of certain Parameters during fermentation of Saimod using Epop: The Traditional Starter for Preparation of Alcoholic beverage by Mishing Tribe of Assam, India.”
- Journal: *Journal of Assam Science Society*.
- Bibliographic details: 52(2):1–6; ISSN 0587-1921; 2011.

### SRC-140 — Epop amylolytic mold
- Authors: Devid Kardong, Kaushal Sood, R.N.S. Yadav and T.C. Bora.
- Title as printed: “Amyolytic activity of mold isolated from Epop used by the Mising tribe of Assam, India in preparation of alcoholic Rice beer.”
- Journal: *Bulletin of Life Sciences*.
- Bibliographic details: Vol. XVI; ISSN 0971-5746; 2011. The institutional listing does not expose the page range.

The institutional bibliography independently confirms that these are two distinct papers, not duplicate titles.

## 2. Evidence boundary

The located PDF is a publication list, not either original article. It does not expose:
- fermentation sampling schedule, measured parameters, values or trends for SRC-139;
- mold/isolate identity, enzyme assay, substrate conditions or activity results for SRC-140;
- sample/batch design, locality, producer or household details for either study;
- page range for SRC-140.

No result is inferred from either title. No fermentation values, organism names, enzyme activities or locality records are added.

## 3. Archive decision

- Keep SRC-139 and SRC-140 as separate primary-study leads.
- Preserve the institutional spelling “Amyolytic” in the title transcription pending inspection of the original paper.
- Keep ASM-GAP-062 **Open / High**.
- Next: obtain the original journal issues or author copies, then extract methods, tables, time-series, organism/isolate identification and limitations. Any locality or sample-level claim requires explicit evidence in the original paper.

**Retrieval status:** Institutional bibliography verified; both original full texts not retrieved.

Do not infer that its isolates, methods or results are the same as the 2007–2009 UGC report or either 2011 paper.
