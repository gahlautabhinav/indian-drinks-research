# SRC-153 — Mili & Sundriyal (2023): Mising ÉPOP starter-culture study

Full text: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s42779-023-00214-2
PDF: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1186/s42779-023-00214-2.pdf

Rajiv Mili and R. C. Sundriyal (2023), “ÉPOP (traditional starter culture): a complex composition of plant resources prepared by the Misings of Assam, Northeast India,” Journal of Ethnic Foods 10, article 47. Published 6 December 2023. DOI 10.1186/s42779-023-00214-2.

## Field design
- Extensive field survey in 15 randomly selected villages in Sivasagar district; two representative villages, Majorbari and Ligiribari, selected for in-depth study.
- Knowledgeable women interviewed formally and informally using a semi-structured questionnaire; collection and preparation observed directly.
- Plant specimens identified with assistance from BSI Arunachal Station, Itanagar; herbarium comparisons involved ARUN and ASSAM regional herbaria and regional floras.
- Acknowledgements name 13 villages: Majorbari, Ligiribari, Afala, Balama, Charaipora (DisangMukh); Dolopa, Balikur, Samukjan, Kotiari, Milanpur (Rajabari); Gumutha, Ramnagar, Balikuthi (Charaideo). This is not a complete list of the 15 surveyed villages.
- Informant/household counts, voucher accession numbers, village-by-taxon matrix and coordinates are not exposed.

## ÉPOP preparation as reported
- Collect plant parts before flowering, wash and sun-dry 3–4 days, then pound.
- Soak glutinous rice 2–3 hours; crush in a traditional rice picker (KI-PAR); mix in pounded plants and crush to a partially powdered mixture.
- Plant ingredients : soaked rice ratio reported as 1:5.
- Shape into oval/round cakes, preferably 20–25 g each, from 1,000 g rice; sprinkle powder from an existing yeast-containing starter cake.
- Prepare two larger reserve cakes called GAI.
- Apply bracken identified by authors as Cyclosorus extensa Nees to freshly prepared cakes to prevent undesirable fungal infestation.
- Dry in sun and over fire for at least seven days; store cool and dry, preferably in earthen KI-LING. Reported storage life up to six months.

This is a starter-cake method, not a complete Nogin or Po:ro beverage fermentation/extraction recipe. The article distinguishes Nogin/Noggin (rice-only substrate in its simplified account) from Poro/Por (ash from rice husk or bran mixed with cooked rice); its introduction describes Poro as distilled. Do not harmonize this with the Ta:suk percolation/filtration methods in SRC-136, SRC-147 and SRC-148.

## Limitations
No complete 15-village list, village-by-taxon matrix, voucher IDs, informant count, microbial profile, biochemical composition or ethanol assay. The authors' harmlessness/remedial claims are not clinical evidence and are not adopted as verified safety or health claims. Preserve the source's taxonomic spellings pending voucher-based reconciliation.
