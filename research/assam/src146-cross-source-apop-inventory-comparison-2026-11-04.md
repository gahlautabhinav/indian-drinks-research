# Mising Apong / Apop plant-list crosswalk — SRC-146 against earlier Mising studies

**Audit date:** 2026-11-04  
**Primary inventories compared:** SRC-146 (Gogoi et al. 2013; Sivasagar), SRC-145 (Handique & Deka 2016; Sivasagar), SRC-136 (Kardong et al. 2012; Dibrugarh), SRC-005 (Mili & Sundriyal 2023; Sivasagar).

## 1. Inventory scope and count integrity

| Source | Scope | Inventory count as source reports it | Extraction status |
|---|---|---:|---|
| SRC-146 | Three named Sivasagar villages; 2012 fieldwork | 39 claimed | 37 visible numbered rows in Table 1; rows 15 and 29 absent |
| SRC-145 | Nine unnamed Sivasagar villages; 2013–14 survey | 17 Mising Apop plants | 17 taxa transcribed in article text |
| SRC-136 | Mising household samples, Dibrugarh district | 16 | 16 Table 1 entries transcribed |
| SRC-005 | 15 Sivasagar villages, in-depth Majorbari/Ligiribari | 31 | 31 Table 1 entries transcribed |

Counts must not be added together or treated as distinct species totals. The sources differ in scope, methods, starter naming, spelling and taxonomic resolution.

These are text-level matches only. Plant parts and local names should still be compared separately; the table does not prove identical household use or use frequency.

### Likely spelling/orthographic variants; not silently merged
- SRC-146 “Capcicum annum” ↔ SRC-145 *Capsicum annuum*.
- SRC-146 “Drymeria cordata” ↔ SRC-145 *Drymaria cordata*.
- SRC-146 “Ptridium aquilinum” ↔ SRC-145 *Pteridium aquilinum*.
- SRC-146 “Phlogacanthus thyrsiformis” ↔ SRC-145 “Phogocanthus thyrsiflorus”. The genus spelling and epithet both vary; this is a candidate requiring botanical verification, not a confirmed synonym.

### Do not merge
- SRC-145 *Zanthoxylum hamiltonianum* is not SRC-146 *Zanthoxylum nitidum*.
- SRC-145 “Ipoemea sp.” cannot be equated with SRC-146 *Ipomoea aquatica* or *Ipomea mauritiana*.
- SRC-145 *Hydrocotyle sibthorpioides* is not SRC-146's *Centella asiatica*; the source has separate taxa and names.
- SRC-145 *Cyclosorus exlensa* is not SRC-146 *Pteridium aquilinum*.

## 5. Interpretation and limitations

1. Repeated names across inventories are useful evidence of recurring reported starter ingredients, but not proof of a standardized statewide recipe.
2. A same-name match does not establish identical plant part, local name, starter role, abundance, seasonality or current use.
3. Ethnomedicinal descriptions in SRC-146 and other studies are community-reported or literature-cited claims, not clinical efficacy.
4. SRC-146's article claims 39 taxa while Table 1 exposes only 37 numbered rows. Do not create two placeholder taxa or inflate the crosswalk.
5. The 2026 Majuli study SRC-084 is a separate 48-taxon, 28-informant field inventory across three unnamed villages. It has its own herbarium submission and explicit separate descriptions for Nogin and ash-added Po:ro Apong. It should be compared as an independent later dataset, not merged with the earlier inventories.
