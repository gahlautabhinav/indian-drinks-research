# SRC-108 audit: Assam starter-culture microbiology study

**Audit date:** 2026-10-24  
**Source:** Saikia, B., Ali, M.S., Gogoi, S.H. & Nath, P.D. (2024). “Isolation and Characterization of the Mycofloral Diversity in Traditional Assamese Alcoholic Fermentation from India.” *Asian Journal of Dairy and Food Research*, 43(1), 104–110. DOI: https://doi.org/10.18805/ajdfr.DR-1829.  
**Readable publisher page:** https://arccjournals.com/journal/asian-journal-of-dairy-and-food-research/DR-1829  
**Full-text mirror:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/359537376_Isolation_and_Characterization_of_the_Mycofloral_Diversity_in_Traditional_Assamese_Alcoholic_Fermentation_from_India

## 1. What the source directly reports

- The abstract names **seven** survey districts: Jorhat, Sivasagar, Golaghat, Lakhimpur, Dhemaji, Majuli and Karbi Anglong.
- The introduction instead describes a survey in **six** districts and nine starter-inoculum samples. This is an internal geographic-count discrepancy.
- Starter samples were collected in 2019–2020 and are described as dry cakes called **Xaaj pitha, Apong, and Suzen (mod pitha)**. The article does not provide, in the accessible sample description, a sample-by-sample table that maps each starter type to a named village, district and community.
- The study isolated 16 yeast strains from nine starter cultures and reports two filamentous fungi. It identifies *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*, *Saccharomycopsis fibuligera*, and *Candida tropicalis* among the yeasts, and *Aspergillus oryzae* and *A. niger* among the filamentous fungi.
- The article says *S. cerevisiae* was dominant across all nine samples. It reports NCBI sequence accessions MK110643 (*S. cerevisiae*), MK110642 (*S. fibuligera*) and MK110644 (*Candida tropicalis*). These are organism-identification results from the study, not a representative microbiome census of every Assam beverage or household.

## 2. Geographic reconciliation

| Claim | Evidence | Audit result |
|---|---|---|
| Seven districts surveyed | Abstract lists Jorhat, Sivasagar, Golaghat, Lakhimpur, Dhemaji, Majuli and Karbi Anglong | Preserve the seven names as the abstract's stated list |
| Six districts surveyed | Introduction says “six different districts” | Contradicts the abstract; unresolved without author correction or underlying sampling records |
| Nine starter samples | Introduction and results refer to nine starter cultures | Sample count is reported, but the sample-to-place mapping is not supplied in the accessible text |
| Xaaj pitha, Apong, Suzen/mod pitha were sampled | Materials and methods | Supports the presence of these starter labels in the study; does not establish a particular community or village for each label |

**Do not** turn the seven district names into seven confirmed production-place records for each starter. Districts describe survey coverage; they do not prove that every starter type was sampled in every district. No new canonical beverage, community or locality records should be created from this paper alone.

## 3. Interpretation limits

1. **Starter cake is not identical to finished beverage.** The sampled materials are described as starter cultures used for brewing. Microbial results from those cakes should not be represented as a direct analysis of every finished beverage.
2. **Taxonomic result is bounded by the study.** The article used culture-based isolation and selected sequencing for representative strains. Its list is not an exhaustive inventory of all microbes in each traditional fermentation.
3. **Risk claims need caution.** The discussion raises concern about *Candida tropicalis*. Detection in a starter-culture study alone does not quantify consumer exposure, establish that all finished products contain it, or demonstrate a clinical health outcome. Preserve the paper's concern as an author interpretation, not as a blanket safety verdict about Assam rice beers.
4. **Publication chronology differs by page.** The publisher record gives volume 43, issue 1 (2024), while the article text states it was first online in March 2022. Cite as the 2024 issue article and retain the online-publication detail only when relevant.

## 4. Recommended next action

Ask the corresponding author or Assam Agricultural University for (a) the original sample register, (b) exact district/locality and community for each of the nine starter cakes, (c) which of the seven abstract districts was omitted from the six-district introduction, and (d) any supplementary table connecting starter type to sample ID. Until resolved, ASM-GAP-053 remains Open.
