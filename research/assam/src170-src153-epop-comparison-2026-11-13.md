# SRC-170 / SRC-153 Mising Epop inventory comparison (canonical source IDs)

**Date:** 13 November 2026  
**Scope:** pairwise comparison of source-transcribed plant lists; not a pooled survey or canonical recipe.

## Sources and comparability

- **SRC-170 — Pawe & Gogoi (2013):** 28 taxon-level names from a table explicitly labelled for Poro Apong/Chai Mod Apob. Field sites are in Lakhimpur and Sonitpur. Two *Thelypteris* names share printed serial 25; serial 26 is absent.
- **SRC-153 — Mili & Sundriyal (2023):** 31 taxa in a general Mising ÉPOP table from Sivasagar. The paper discusses both Poro and Nogin Apong but does not allocate individual taxa to either variant.

This is therefore a comparison of a Poro-specific inventory against a general ÉPOP inventory—not two Poro recipes.

## Exact lexical overlap

Four exact lexical matches were confirmed after stripping author citations and punctuation:

| Name | SRC-170 plant part | SRC-153 plant part |
|---|---|---|
| *Lygodium flexuosum* | Stem & leaf | Foliar part / young shoot |
| *Piper longum* | Dry inflorescence | Leaf |
| *Psidium guajava* | Leaf | Leaf / young shoot |
| *Saccharum officinarum* | Leaf | Leaf |

The differences in plant-part reporting are material. In particular, *Piper longum* is not documented as the same ingredient preparation in the two tables.

## Candidate correspondence not merged

- SRC-170 prints “Scoperia dulcis”; SRC-153 prints *Scoparia dulcis*. This is a plausible spelling correspondence, but this pass does not treat it as an exact lexical match or accepted-name determination. Verify against the source image and an authoritative botanical name record before normalizing.
- SRC-170 prints *Artocarpus heterophylla*; SRC-153 prints *Artocarpus heterophyllus*. Similar spelling is not enough to assert that the source records refer to the same identified plant.
- SRC-153's *Selaginella* sp. is not promoted to SRC-170's *Selaginella semicordata*. A genus-only identification cannot validate a species-level match.
- Similar genera or epithets such as *Spilanthes calva* / *Spilanthes acmella* and *Piper nigrum* / *Piper longum* remain distinct.

## Interpretation limits

1. The four lexical overlaps do not show that the plants are used by all Mising households or at equal frequency.
2. They do not establish a shared Poro/Nogin recipe. SRC-153 has no variant allocation.
3. The district difference (Lakhimpur/Sonitpur versus Sivasagar), publication gap and different inventory designs preclude treating the two tables as a longitudinal trend.
4. SRC-153's qualitative sourcing-dependency fields cannot be projected onto SRC-170's villages.
5. No plant-by-village, household prevalence, harvest volume or ecological abundance claims are supported.

## Source-ID control

SRC-172 was a duplicate registration of the same Mili & Sundriyal article already held as SRC-153. This comparison uses the canonical ID SRC-153; SRC-172 is an alias only and is not counted as an independent study.
