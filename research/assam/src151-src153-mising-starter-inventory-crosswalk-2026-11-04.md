# SRC-151 ↔ SRC-153 — Mising starter-plant inventory crosswalk

**Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Sources:** Duarah, Kalita & Mishra (2023), Sibsagar Mising Opo Pitha/Apob inventory (SRC-151); Mili & Sundriyal (2023), Sivasagar ÉPOP inventory (SRC-153).

## Scope and method
SRC-151's 36 distinct numbered table entries were transcribed from the publisher PDF. Its rows 22 and 23 are physically printed twice; the CSV includes each once and records the anomaly. SRC-153 reports 31 taxa in Table 1. Comparison here uses the first two scientific-name tokens (binomial/genus-level string) as a preliminary crosswalk, while retaining each source's printed spelling separately. This is not a taxonomic synonym check or proof of same population, plant specimen, part, or recipe.

## Additional review candidates (not counted as confirmed overlaps)
- **Lygodium flexuosum:** SRC-153 lists *Lygodium flexuosum*; SRC-151 row 3 gives “Lygodium japonicum/ L. Flexuosum” as a combined alternative. This is a plausible name-level overlap, but SRC-151 does not resolve which of the two species was used.
- **Polygonum plebeium / Polygonum plebium:** SRC-153 prints *Polygonum plebeium*; SRC-151 prints *Polygonum plebium*. Treat as spelling-level candidate only; do not silently normalize.
- **Spilanthes acmella / Spilanthes calva:** SRC-153 lists *Spilanthes acmella*; SRC-151 row 19 has “Spilanthes calva” in the botanical-name cell and “Spilanthes acmella (L.)” in the adjacent family cell. The source's table layout is anomalous; no single corrected identification is imposed.
- No synonym-level equivalences are asserted for other superficially related names.

## Differences and evidence boundaries
- The seven shared binomial strings are not a statewide or community-wide consensus list. The two studies have different field designs and likely different sample coverage; SRC-151's exact villages and informant count are not stated in its article, while SRC-153 reports 15 villages and in-depth work in Majorbari and Ligiribari.
- Both are 2023 studies, but they are separate publications and their data should not be treated as independent replications without comparing methods, field dates and possible overlap in informants/locations.
- The papers use inconsistent family assignments for *Hydrocotyle sibthorpioides*; preserve both as printed pending voucher-level resolution.
- SRC-151's plant table is for Mising Opo Pitha/Apob and the article distinguishes Nogin from Poro Apong; SRC-153 focuses on ÉPOP starter preparation and also distinguishes Nogin and Poro. Do not use plant overlap to infer a shared beverage process.
- No medicinal claims from SRC-151 are validated by this crosswalk; ethnobotanical claims remain attributed reports.
