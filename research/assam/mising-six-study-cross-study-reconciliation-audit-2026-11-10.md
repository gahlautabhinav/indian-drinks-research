# Six-study Mising Apong / Apob / E’pob starter-plant reconciliation

**Audit date:** 10 November 2026  
**Studies:** SRC-007, SRC-146, SRC-153, SRC-160, SRC-161 and SRC-170.  
**Purpose:** add the newly recovered Poro-specific Pawe & Gogoi (2013) Apob inventory to the earlier five-study comparison without treating variant-specific lists as interchangeable.

## Six-study scopes

- **SRC-007 — Yein et al. (2022), Majuli:** 21 visible Epop table entries; article reports survey Sep–Dec 2021 and more than 20 village households visited, but names no villages or assigns plants to Po:ro/Nagin.
- **SRC-146 — Gogoi, Dutta & Mondal (2013), Sivasagar:** 37 visible numbered rows (article claims 39); combines three-village field work with literature-derived material.
- **SRC-153 — Mili & Sundriyal (2023), Sivasagar:** 31 taxa; 15 villages surveyed and in-depth study at Majorbari and Ligiribari; herbarium comparisons are reported but accession-level and village-by-taxon data remain incomplete.
- **SRC-160 — Gogoi et al. (2026), Jorhat:** 20 taxa in the Po:ro subsection; conclusion attributes starter plants collectively to Po:ro and Nogin, so do not treat the list as Po:ro-exclusive. Ten villages and 50 key informants reported, but beverage-specific sample allocation and village-level plant mapping absent.
- **SRC-161 — Borah (2026), Majuli:** 48 numbered E’pob entries from three unnamed villages and 28 informants; no plant-to-variant allocation. *Zanthoxylum oxyphyllum* repeats at rows 20 and 33.
- **SRC-170 — Pawe & Gogoi (2013), Lakhimpur and Sonitpur:** 28 taxon strings in a Poro-specific Apob table, transcribed from 27 visible serials. Seven selected villages are named: Dhunaguri, Badati, Bongalmora, Chamaguri Temona, Luitmukh, Kathalkuchi and Rajbari. The authors report interviews with regular Poro brewers, direct observation, plant collection and identification confirmation at BSI Shillong (ASSAM) and Itanagar (ARUN). No field dates, respondent count, voucher accession numbers or plant-by-village matrix are reported. See `src170-pawe-gogoi-2013-poro-apong-acquisition-audit.md`.

## Data products

1. `mising-six-study-source-row-inventory.csv`: 185 source-row/taxon observations across the six studies (21 + 37 + 31 + 20 + 48 + 28).
2. `mising-six-study-exact-name-crosswalk.csv`: 117 lexical taxon keys, generated by removing parenthetical author citations, punctuation and hyphens, then comparing the first two name tokens.
3. `src170-pawe-gogoi-2013-poro-apob-28-taxon-inventory.csv`: the source-faithful 28-taxon SRC-170 transcription; row 25a/25b split one table cell containing two taxa while retaining the same original table serial.

## SRC-170-specific observations

- The Poro-specific inventory adds four exact lexical overlaps with SRC-007, eight with SRC-146, four with SRC-153, eight with SRC-160 and seven with SRC-161.
- Shared exact labels across SRC-170 and other studies include taxa such as *Centella asiatica*, *Clerodendrum viscosum*, *Lygodium flexuosum*, *Oryza sativa*, *Piper longum*, *Psidium guajava*, *Saccharum officinarum*, *Scoparia/Scoperia dulcis*, *Zingiber officinale* and others, but only those with matching first two tokens count in the lexical crosswalk. Source spellings and botanical author citations remain preserved in the long-form inventory.
- SRC-170 explicitly assigns its own table to Poro Apong Apob. This is stronger variant-specific evidence than the mixed/collective inventories in some other papers; it must not be projected onto those papers.
- Similar but non-identical taxa are not merged: *Lygodium flexuosum* is distinct from *Lygodium japonicum*; *Drymaria diandra* is distinct from *Drymaria cordata*; *Spilanthes calva* is not treated as *Spilanthes acmella* or *Acmella paniculata*.
- The taxonomic-authority review is separate: `mising-five-study-taxonomic-authority-review-2026-11-10.md`. POWO synonym relationships must not overwrite source strings.

## Limitations and next work

1. The SRC-170 table has 27 visible serials; serial 26 is absent. Its serial 25 contains two Thelypteris names. The article's stated 28-species total matches 28 taxon strings if both names in row 25 are counted separately, but the skipped serial is unexplained.
2. The source inventories are not harmonized for variant scope: SRC-170 is explicitly Poro-specific; SRC-160's list is collective across Poro and Nogin; SRC-007/SRC-161 do not map individual taxa to variant; SRC-153 covers ÉPOP context. Keep those allocation fields visible.
3. SRC-170's village list does not map individual plants to villages. Do not create plant-village edges or map pins from the study-wide list.
4. Obtain voucher accessions, original herbarium labels and author clarifications for SRC-153, SRC-161 and SRC-170 before resolving remaining ambiguous spellings or specimen identity.
5. Do not create a canonical universal Mising starter recipe from summed inventories. The source-row counts are observations in different study designs, districts, periods and beverage contexts.

## Archive decision

The six-study inventory and lexical crosswalk are research aids only. No canonical plant, village, community or beverage records were created or merged. ASM-GAP-068, ASM-GAP-069 and ASM-GAP-070 remain open for their respective voucher, field-matrix and variant-allocation gaps; ASM-GAP-060 remains open for SRC-170's missing field/voucher metadata and table-numbering clarification.
