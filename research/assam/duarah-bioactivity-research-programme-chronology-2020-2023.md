## Plant-set comparison (source strings only)

- 2020 conference abstract vs AAU thesis abstract: three exact source strings overlap — *Sphaerostepheras unitus*, *Drymaria cordata*, *Elsholtzia blanda*. The fourth 2020 string “Xanthozylum nitidium” resembles thesis “Zanthoxylum nitidium”, but this spelling difference is not silently normalized.
- AAU thesis abstract lists 12 plants: *Curcuma longa*, *Sphaerostepheras unitus*, *Polygonum hydropiper*, *Drymaria cordata*, *Psidium guajava*, *Cinnamomum bejolghota*, *Zanthoxylum nitidium*, *Elsholtzia blanda*, *Rubus hexagonus*, *Vitex negundo*, *Justicia gendarussa* and *Scoparia dulcis*.
- SRC-154's result paragraph explicitly names five distinct plant strings in its *E. coli* results: “Cucurmin longa”, *Polygonum hydropiper*, *Drymaria cordata*, *Psidium guajava* and *Vitex negundo*. The abstract says six plants were screened but does not identify the sixth in the accessible text. Do not fill that gap by borrowing from the thesis list.
- The thesis abstract and SRC-154 share several plant strings and the same broad antimicrobial target set, but this does not prove identical samples or independent experiments. The 2020 abstract and thesis likewise likely represent related programme outputs, but sample and assay overlap cannot be confirmed from abstracts alone.

## Numerical evidence available from the thesis abstract only

The AAU thesis abstract reports:
- Proximate ranges across selected plants: moisture 4–10.5 g/100 g; ash 6.09–16.61 g/100 g; crude fibre 6.12–13.40 g/100 g; protein 0.23±0.10–6.81±0.45 g/100 g; crude fat 0.60–6.22±0.36 g/100 g; carbohydrate 69.91–85.85±0.16 g/100 g.
- ABTS: aqueous *Psidium guajava* leaf extract 77% scavenging at 200 µg/mL, IC50 74.05 µg/mL; ethanolic *Curcuma longa* extract 87.4%, IC50 68.52 µg/mL.
- DPPH: aqueous *Psidium guajava* 65.22%, IC50 113.25 µg/mL; ethanolic *Curcuma longa* 70.6%, IC50 99.02 µg/mL.
- Total flavonoids, total phenols, tannins and alkaloids are reported as ranges in the abstract; *Polygonum hydropiper* is reported with highest alkaloid content.
- Antimicrobial results include *E. coli*, *Listeria monocytogenes*, *Salmonella typhi* and *Serratia marcescens* zones. The abstract reports no activity against *Shigella*.

These are abstract-transcribed results. Units and decimal formatting are retained as reported; the full thesis is needed to verify methods, plant-by-plant tables, concentrations, controls, replication and statistical treatment.

## Interpretation safeguards

1. Do not call these tests “rice beer antimicrobial assays”: the methods describe plant extracts, not finished beverage samples.
2. Do not infer human efficacy, therapeutic benefit, safety or clinical outcomes from in-vitro assays.
3. Do not add the 2020 and 2023 abstracts as separate independent experiments to any study count until full methods/specimens/raw data are compared.
4. Do not treat the thesis abstract's 12-plant assay set as the same as the complete 36-entry Mising inventory or the 51-plant multi-community survey.
5. Preserve “Sibsagar” as the thesis/article source spelling where it appears; do not silently rewrite the study's geographic scope.
