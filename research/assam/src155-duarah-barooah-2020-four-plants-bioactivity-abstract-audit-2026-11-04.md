# SRC-155 — Duarah & Barooah (2020) four-plant rice-beer starter bioactivity abstract

**Citation:** Radali Duarah and Mridula Saikia Barooah (2020). “Bioactivity of Four Important Plants Used in Traditional Rice Beer Starter Culture of Assam.” 9th International Congress on Biotechnology and Food Sciences, SciTech BioTech-Food Sciences 2020, Thailand. Received 4 February 2020; accepted 7 February 2020; published 7 February 2020 (web abstract record).

**Source:** SciTech Central accepted-abstract record: https://www.scitcentralconferences.com/accepteddetails/international-congress-on-biotechnology-and-food-sciences/759

## Abstract-reported study

The abstract names four plants said to be commonly used in traditional rice-beer starter culture in Assam:
1. *Sphaerostepheras unitus*
2. *Drymaria cordata*
3. *Elsholtzia blanda*
4. “Xanthozylum nitidium” (source spelling; not silently corrected)

It states that:
- phytochemical screening was conducted;
- antioxidant evaluation used DPPH (printed “DPPPH”) and ABTS assays;
- quantitative phytochemical analysis followed “standard protocols” (protocols not specified);
- ethanolic and methanolic extracts were screened for antibacterial activity by agar-disc-diffusion;
- five targets were named: *Salmonella typhi*, *Escherichia coli*, *Listeria monocytogenes*, *Shigella* and *Serratia* (no *Serratia* species specified);
- the abstract reports classes including reducing sugars, cardiac glycosides, phenolic compounds, flavonoids and alkaloids.

## What the abstract does not provide

No plant-part details, sample provenance, district/village, voucher numbers, extraction concentrations, bacterial strain identifiers, positive/negative controls, replicate counts, inhibition-zone measurements, antioxidant values, IC50 values, statistical tests or full analytical protocol are reported in the accessible abstract. The text uses broad prospective language about therapeutic-agent development; this is not evidence of clinical efficacy.

## Relationship to the Duarah thesis and later abstracts

This abstract is by the same researcher and adviser associated with the AAU PhD thesis “Bioactivity of medicinal plants used in traditional rice beer starter cultures of Assam” (listed in the AAU *Post Graduate Thesis 2020–21* compilation, pp. 350–351), SRC-154 (Duarah's 2023 NSI antimicrobial abstract) and SRC-151 (Duarah, Kalita & Mishra 2023 ethnobotanical survey).

The four-plant set overlaps with the later thesis's 12-plant set at least by the source strings *Sphaerostepheras unitus*, *Drymaria cordata* and *Elsholtzia blanda*. The fourth abstract string “Xanthozylum nitidium” resembles the thesis string “Zanthoxylum nitidium”, but the source discrepancy is preserved and not treated as a verified taxonomic determination.

The 2020 abstract likely documents an earlier stage or subset of the same research programme. The accessible sources do not establish whether it reports the same specimens, assays or raw data as the thesis or SRC-154. Do not count the abstracts as independent experiments, pool values, or infer that the 2020 abstract's four plants were all sampled in the same district/community.

## Research handling

- Keep the four plant strings and assay claims source-faithful.
- Do not add numerical laboratory data because none are present.
- Treat all assays as in-vitro plant-extract work, not testing of finished Apong or starter cakes and not evidence of beverage safety or human health effects.
