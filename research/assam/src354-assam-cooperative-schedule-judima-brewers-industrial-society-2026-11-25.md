# SRC-354 — Assam cooperative schedule entry for Judima Traditional Brewers Industrial C.S. Ltd.

**Audit date:** 25 November 2026

## Source and identification

**Document:** Appendix A in a 69-page PDF hosted by the Assam Legislative Assembly Session Archive (alaesession.org), file name `answer510.pdf`.  
**URL:** https://alaesession.org/assets/uploads/answer/answer510.pdf  
**Relevant location:** PDF page 40 of 69, serial number 473 (text extraction lines 1766–1768 of the opened PDF).

The schedule's table headings are:
- Serial number
- Name of Sub Division
- Name of Societies
- Permission Accorded 2020–21
- Permission Not Accorded 2020–21
- Permission Accorded 2021–22
- Permission Not Accorded 2021–22
- Remarks

Entry 473 reads:
- Subdivision: Haflong
- Society name: “Judima Traditional Brewers Industrial C.S.Ltd.”
- Permission accorded 2020–21: “Do” (continuation of “Accorded” in preceding rows)
- Permission accorded 2021–22: “Do” (continuation of “Accorded”)
- No remark or registration number is shown in this row.

The document extraction does not expose the schedule's full explanatory heading/question in English. Therefore the type/nature of the “permission” is **not identified** in this audit. Do not call it an alcohol manufacturing licence, operating licence, renewal, registration certificate or GI authorisation. The two columns only show that the schedule records permission as accorded for those two financial years.

## Link to the cooperative reported in other sources

The name is a close documentary match to the “Judima Traditional Brewers' Industrial Co-operative Society Ltd (JTBICS)” named in:
- Chatterjee (2024), *Open*, SRC-352, quoting Sarbojit Thaosen as a member/stakeholder and describing cooperative standards and bottling;
- Goswami & Sarma (2023), *The Jugaad Project*, SRC-353, identifying the same named cooperative as a women's cooperative production channel.

The Assembly schedule's shorter form “C.S.Ltd.” plausibly expands to “Co-operative Society Ltd.” However, without a registration number or the society's registration certificate/by-laws, this audit treats the matching identity as **strongly indicated but not fully legally crosswalked**. Preserve the exact source spelling and the expanded journalistic/academic name separately.

## What this adds

This is the first located documentary record placing the named Judima brewers' industrial cooperative in a formal Assam cooperative schedule:
- Haflong subdivision;
- listed at serial 473;
- permission marked accorded in both 2020–21 and 2021–22 columns.

It is not a current status check. The schedule is historical and the file is hosted on a third-party archive of legislative material, not the live Registrar of Cooperative Societies' individual-society record.

## What remains unknown

The source does not provide:
- registration number/date;
- registered office street/village;
- date of incorporation;
- society category/classification;
- current functional status;
- registered bye-laws or objects;
- current board/office bearers;
- number or gender composition of members;
- individual producer members or supplying households;
- source villages for cooperative supply;
- annual production, bottling capacity or sales;
- GI authorised-user registration status;
- what specific permission was accorded for 2020–21 and 2021–22.

The Assam Registrar of Cooperative Societies' current public pages describe a statewide cooperative registry and provide general society counts, but the accessible pages reviewed in this pass did not provide a searchable individual record for this society. The NCDC cooperative search interface was also not retrievable in this pass. Do not infer that the society is absent or inactive from these access limitations.

## Archive decisions

1. Register this schedule as SRC-354.
2. Link the Haflong subdivision as a cooperative-record location only; do not convert it into a Judima production village.
3. Keep the exact name “Judima Traditional Brewers Industrial C.S.Ltd.” as the schedule's transcription, and the expanded JTBICS form as the SRC-352/353 source spelling.
4. Do not interpret the permission columns beyond the literal “accorded” status for the two named years.
5. Next documentary target: obtain the society's registration certificate, registration number/date, registered bye-laws, latest audit/return and current functional-status extract from the Assistant Registrar of Cooperative Societies, Haflong / Registrar of Cooperative Societies, Assam. No request or contact has been made.
