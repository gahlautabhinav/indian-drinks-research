Entry 473 reads:

The document extraction does not expose the schedule's full explanatory heading/question in English. Therefore the type/nature of the “permission” is **not identified** in this audit. Do not call it an alcohol manufacturing licence, operating licence, renewal, registration certificate or GI authorisation. The two columns only show that the schedule records permission as accorded for those two financial years.

## Link to the cooperative reported in other sources

The name is a close documentary match to the “Judima Traditional Brewers' Industrial Co-operative Society Ltd (JTBICS)” named in:
- Chatterjee (2024), *Open*, SRC-352, quoting Sarbojit Thaosen as a member/stakeholder and describing cooperative standards and bottling;
- Goswami & Sarma (2023), *The Jugaad Project*, SRC-353, identifying the same named cooperative as a women's cooperative production channel.

The Assembly schedule's shorter form “C.S.Ltd.” plausibly expands to “Co-operative Society Ltd.” However, without a registration number or the society's registration certificate/by-laws, this audit treats the matching identity as **strongly indicated but not fully legally crosswalked**. Preserve the exact source spelling and the expanded journalistic/academic name separately.

## What this adds

This is the first located documentary record placing the named Judima brewers' industrial cooperative in a formal Assam cooperative schedule:
- Haflong subdivision;
- listed at serial 473;
- permission marked accorded in both 2020–21 and 2021–22 columns.

It is not a current status check. The schedule is historical and the file is hosted on a third-party archive of legislative material, not the live Registrar of Cooperative Societies' individual-society record.

## Historical development context — SRC-355 (Bathari, 2020)

Uttam Bathari's 16 January 2020 NEZINE article says YADEM and Dibarai Mahila Samity organised a Judima festival in Haflong in December 2016. It describes the festival's commercialisation objective as exploring cooperative production of Judima with women's involvement, drawing an analogy to cooperative dairy production in Gujarat. This is valuable evidence of the **community/cooperative model being proposed and discussed by 2016**, but it does not say that JTBICS was already incorporated, name JTBICS, or provide a registration date. The later society name appears in the cooperative schedule and 2023/2024 articles. Thus the safest timeline is:

- Dec 2016: YADEM + Dibarai Mahila Samity festival; women's cooperative production discussed as an objective (SRC-355).
- 2020–21 and 2021–22: “Judima Traditional Brewers Industrial C.S.Ltd.” appears in a Haflong-subdivision schedule with permission marked accorded for both years (SRC-354; nature of permission not legible).
- 2023: Goswami & Sarma identify the expanded JTBICS name as a women's cooperative production channel (SRC-353).
- Jul 2024: Open quotes JTBICS stakeholder Sarbojit Thaosen on cooperative quality standards and bottling (SRC-352).

This sequence does not establish the incorporation date or prove that the 2016 proposed cooperative and the society listed later are legally the same body. It is a plausible institutional development path that requires the registration certificate and bye-laws to confirm.

## Addendum — 25 November 2026: official RCS jurisdiction and schedule interpretation

A closer reading of the opened original PDF confirms that serial 473 has “Do” in both permission-accorded columns; this is a continuation marker for “Accorded” in the preceding row. The column headings are “Permission Accorded 2020–21” and “Permission Accorded 2021–22.” The nature of the permission is still not identified, and must not be described as an excise, manufacturing or operating licence.

The official Assam RCS directory places Dima Hasao under Haflong and lists Haflong/Maibong subdivisions. The registration responsibility page distinguishes district-level cooperatives (District Deputy Registrar) from other primary cooperatives (Assistant Registrar). The RCS directory lists Longbir Teron as District Deputy Registrar at Haflong; official pages disagree on the named Assistant Registrar at Haflong. JTBICS's classification and area of operation are unknown, so the competent record custodian cannot yet be fixed. See SRC-356.

The RCS public list is aggregate (369 industrial societies statewide) and its downloadable named list is for state-level societies. No accessible named record for JTBICS is exposed there. This does not establish that the society is unregistered or inactive.
