# Assam rice-beer literature audit — additional 2011–2016 sources (2026-11-04)

## Newly identified records
- **SRC-138 — Arjun, Verma & Prasad (2014), Judima.** Cotton University faculty bibliography provides full citation (International Food Research Journal 21(2):463–470) and a direct PDF link. Treat as an independent primary preparation/biochemical study; detailed extraction remains pending.
- **SRC-139 — Kardong (2011), Saimod fermentation parameters.** Dibrugarh University research-publication record lists “Studies on the Changes of certain Parameters during fermentation of Saimod using Epop,” Journal of Assam Science Society 52(2):1–6. Bibliography only; do not infer results.
- **SRC-140 — Kardong et al. (2011), Epop amylolytic mold.** Same institutional record lists a distinct Bulletin of Life Sciences Vol. XVI paper. Full page range and article not yet retrieved.
- **SRC-141 — Das et al. (2016), in-vitro polyphenol antioxidant study.** Full article reports leaf samples of *Artocarpus heterophyllus*, *Oldenlandia corymbosa*, *Cyclosorus extensa* and *Alpinia malaccensis* collected from Tezpur University botanical garden in summer 2013; HPLC and ten in-vitro antioxidant assays were used. These results do not establish clinical benefit, beverage-level efficacy or the use of each plant in a particular community's starter.
- **SRC-142 — Tezpur University/MoFPI 2012 final R&D project report.** Government inventory confirms project title, institution, completion and aims. The original report remains unlocated; project-wide scope must not be conflated with household preparation.

## Evidence boundary
The systematic review's 42 included studies are not automatically 42 missing archive records. These five leads were separately checked against the current register before being added. The 2011 records are bibliography-confirmed only; SRC-138 has a full-PDF link but has not yet been transcribed; SRC-141 was read in full; SRC-142 is a government inventory entry rather than the original report.
