# Luk Lao: named locality and identity audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-24  
**Source:** SRC-117, Chatterjee (2024), Open Magazine, “Brewing Heritage”  
**Status:** Provisional beverage lead; identity boundary unresolved

## New evidence

A 12 July 2024 feature reports that a commercial production unit for a rice brew called **Luk Lao** was established in **Demou village, Sivasagar district**. The account says local Ahom and Kalita people were employed in preparation and that the brand launched in 2022. The report attributes the account to the producer and places it in the wider Assam heritage-liquor context. This is a named locality and commercial production lead, not an independently audited recipe or a census of household brewing.

Source: https://openthemagazine.com/art-culture/brewing-heritage

## Identity decision

Keep Luk Lao as a **provisional separate archive record** so its named commercial history and locality are discoverable. Do not merge it with Xajpani, Sajpani, Nam-Lau or Lao-pani solely from spelling resemblance. Existing sources associate these names with Tai-Ahom beverages but do not establish that all refer to the same product, process, or register of use. The feature gives no sufficiently detailed preparation protocol to compare the products technically.

## Evidence limits

- Named locality: Demou, Sivasagar district, in the 2024 reporting.
- Reported commercial launch: 2022; producer says he applied for a licence the preceding year and was the first licensee. Treat the “first” claim as attributed, not independently verified.
- The feature does not provide a full ingredient list, starter-cake name, fermentation schedule, equipment specification, or laboratory profile for Luk Lao.
- Employing Ahom and Kalita workers does not by itself establish a single exclusive community origin.
- No village-wide or current household prevalence claim is supported.

## Next checks

Find primary reporting, licence/heritage-liquor records, producer documentation, or consent-based community testimony that describes Luk Lao's preparation and relationship, if any, to Xajpani/Sajpani/Laopani. Keep the distinction unresolved until evidence is explicit.
