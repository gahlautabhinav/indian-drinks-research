# Luk Lao: named locality and identity audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-24  
**Source:** SRC-117, Chatterjee (2024), Open Magazine, “Brewing Heritage”  
**Status:** Provisional beverage lead; identity boundary unresolved

## New evidence

A 12 July 2024 feature reports that a commercial production unit for a rice brew called **Luk Lao** was established in **Demou village, Sivasagar district**. The account says local Ahom and Kalita people were employed in preparation and that the brand launched in 2022. The report attributes the account to the producer and places it in the wider Assam heritage-liquor context. This is a named locality and commercial production lead, not an independently audited recipe or a census of household brewing.

Source: https://openthemagazine.com/art-culture/brewing-heritage

## Identity decision

Keep Luk Lao as a **provisional separate archive record** so its named commercial history and locality are discoverable. Do not merge it with Xajpani, Sajpani, Nam-Lau or Lao-pani solely from spelling resemblance. Existing sources associate these names with Tai-Ahom beverages but do not establish that all refer to the same product, process, or register of use. The feature gives no sufficiently detailed preparation protocol to compare the products technically.

## Evidence limits

- Named locality: Demou, Sivasagar district, in the 2024 reporting.
- Reported commercial launch: 2022; producer says he applied for a licence the preceding year and was the first licensee. Treat the “first” claim as attributed, not independently verified.
- The feature does not provide a full ingredient list, starter-cake name, fermentation schedule, equipment specification, or laboratory profile for Luk Lao.
- Employing Ahom and Kalita workers does not by itself establish a single exclusive community origin.
- No village-wide or current household prevalence claim is supported.

## Next checks

Find primary reporting, licence/heritage-liquor records, producer documentation, or consent-based community testimony that describes Luk Lao's preparation and relationship, if any, to Xajpani/Sajpani/Laopani. Keep the distinction unresolved until evidence is explicit.


## Regulatory follow-up (2026-10-24)

A search located the Government of Assam's 6 May 2017 Gazette amendment, Part IX, **Heritage Alcoholic Beverages**, reproduced at India Code (SRC-118). The indexed official extract defines a “starter cake” as a microbial culture used to ferment a substrate, prepared from grain, leaves, flowers, seeds and other plant parts through traditional methods and processes. This supports the existence of a specific regulatory framework for commercial heritage alcoholic beverages, but it does **not** verify Luk Lao's licence, exact product specification, current operating status, or identity relationship to Xajpani/Sajpani/Laopani. The official PDF endpoint timed out during direct retrieval; the precise rules and any licence-specific records still need document-level confirmation.

The same Open Magazine article separately discusses the Luk Lao and Xaj commercial brands. That editorial distinction establishes two reported commercial product names, not necessarily two distinct underlying traditional beverage categories. A 2007 Tai-Ahom ethnobotanical study describes Sajpani (Tai) and Laopani (Assamese), but does not mention Luk Lao. Therefore the archive continues to keep Luk Lao provisional and separate pending product-level comparison and community/producer confirmation.

Sources: [SRC-117](https://openthemagazine.com/art-culture/brewing-heritage); [SRC-118](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_AS_58_531_00001_00001_1544168097283&filename=no._164_ex.138-2015-32_dated_06-05-17_heritage_liquor.pdf&type=rule); Saikia, Tag & Das (2007), [Tai-Ahom beverage study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/237772274_Ethnobotany_of_foods_and_beverages_among_the_rural_farmers_of_Tai_Ahom_of_North_Lakhimpur_district_Asom).

## 2026-10-28 — Official licensing-route check (SRC-126)

The Government of Assam's Administrative Reforms and Training Department lists notified public services for heritage alcoholic beverages, including grant of a Heritage Alcoholic Beverages Micro-Brewery License, wholesale/retail licence services and renewals. The Assam State Excise Revenue Management System also exposes a licence-verification form that requests a file reference or licence number.

- Service catalogue: https://art.assam.gov.in/portlet-innerpage/artps-list-of-notified-public-services
- Licence verification portal: https://www.stateexcise.assam.gov.in/index.php/Paycollection/verifylicense
- SRC-126 records the service catalogue as an official administrative lead.

**What this establishes:** there is an official service/verification route relevant to investigating commercial heritage-beverage licensing.

**What it does not establish:** neither page names Luk Lao, Demou, the producer or a licence number; neither proves that a licence was granted, is current, or covers the reported product/site. Do not infer licence status from the existence of a licensing service or from the producer's statement in SRC-117.

**Next evidence request:** ask the producer or Assam Excise Department for the exact licence/file reference and a copy of the relevant manufactory, micro-brewery, brand/label or sales authorisation. Verify the reference through the official portal where supported. Independently obtain a product/process description and consent-based Tai-Ahom/community evidence before deciding whether Luk Lao is distinct from or related to Xajpani/Sajpani/Laopani. ASM-GAP-054 remains Open.
