# SRC-128 source audit — South Assam household beverage study

**Audit date:** 2026-10-31  
**Source:** Hanna Yumnam, Soumitra Nath and Indu Sharma (first published online 29 December 2025; issue June 2026), “Brewing Traditions of Northeast India, South Assam: Exploring Rice Beer's Cultural Significance and Indigenous Craftsmanship,” *Biotechnology and Applied Biochemistry* 73(3):1501–1511. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/bab.70113.

## Verified from publisher and PubMed metadata

- Original research article; PubMed PMID 41459787.
- Fieldwork period: May–October 2023.
- Study geography: Cachar, Hailakandi and Sribhumi (current district name; historically Karimganj).
- Five communities: Dimasa Kachari, Hmar, Zeme Naga, Karbi and Tea tribes.
- Abstract reports 49 household visits and interviews with elderly participants aged 50–70.
- Abstract names *Acacia pennata*, *Glycyrrhiza glabra* and *Croton joufra* among plants used, and gives “Ndhui gei” as an unidentified local plant name.
- The publisher's data availability statement says the author will make information available on a justifiable request.

## Limits and archive treatment

The publisher-accessible HTML inspected for this pass exposes the abstract, article metadata and references, but not the full methods, tables, named villages, household-level data or community-by-community plant attribution. The abstract does not allocate each community, beverage, plant or household to a specific district or village.

Accordingly:
1. Register as a new original source (SRC-128), distinct from the 2024 southern Assam metagenomics study (SRC-110) and the 2026 Bodo/Rabha/Ahom cultural ecology study (SRC-124).
2. Do not create new place rows from the three-district study frame alone; district-level coverage for Cachar/Hailakandi/Sribhumi is already represented in the archive and needs beverage-specific allocation.
3. Do not assign the three named plant taxa to all five communities or to any one beverage without the full paper's table-level evidence.
4. Keep “Ndhui gei” as an unresolved source-reported plant name, not a taxonomic identification.
5. Do not convert ritual/medicinal/social descriptions into clinical health claims.

## Next retrieval

Request the full article and any supplementary tables / field-site allocation from the corresponding author, specifically:
- community × district × beverage allocation;
- exact village/household counts and anonymized locality data;
- plant local names, accepted taxa, parts used, and beverage/starter attribution;
- source-specific starter names and preparation methods;
- clarification of “Ndhui gei”.

**Decision:** SRC-128 expands the evidence register but does not yet justify canonical beverage, locality, ingredient or process changes.
