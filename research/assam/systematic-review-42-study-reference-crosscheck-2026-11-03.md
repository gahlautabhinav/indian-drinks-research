# Assam systematic-review cross-check — 2026-11-03

## Review
Barman, P., Roy, K., & Sahu, R. (2026), “Systematic review on traditional fermented rice beverages of Assam,” Next Research 4, article 101241. DOI: 10.1016/j.nexres.2025.101241. Publisher preview reports 42 included studies from 2,997 initial search results. This is a secondary synthesis, not a new field census.

## New primary-source leads
- **SRC-135 — Pawe & Gogoi (2013), “Ethnobotany of Poro Apong or Chai Mod,” NeBIO 4(2):46–49.** Bibliography corroborated by BSI and review citations. Full text not retrieved; no locality, informant count or taxa extracted.
- **SRC-136 — Kardong et al. (2012), “Evaluation of Nutritional and Biochemical aspects of Po:ro apong (Saimod),” IJTK 11(3):499–504.** Abstract reports 16 Epop plant species, burnt rice-husk/rice-straw ash, and ethanol 7.52–18.5% v/v. Full methods, plant list, locality, batch count and analytical design remain unverified.
- **SRC-137 — MoFPI/Tezpur University R&D project (2012; government summary 2017).** Reports starter-cake development and laboratory-standardized rice beer. This is project-level/experimental evidence, not a household traditional recipe.

## Archive changes
Added SRC-135–137, linked them to ASM-003-POR, and added ASM-GAP-060 to retrieve the two Poro Apong primary articles and reconcile their inventories with later Mising research. A structured CSV records the evidence extracted and outstanding fields.

## Integrity notes
The review says 42 studies were included, but that does not mean 42 studies are missing from the archive. Its full inclusion list and reference-to-inclusion mapping remain unavailable from the publisher preview (direct page access returned 403). Do not estimate missing-study counts. Distinguish ethnomedical claims, in-vitro findings, predicted microbial functions and human clinical evidence.
