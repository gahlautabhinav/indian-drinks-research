# E’pob taxonomy follow-up: Acmella / Spilanthes naming conflict

**Audit date:** 2026-10-24  
**Gap:** ASM-GAP-045  
**Scope:** Recheck one candidate overlap between the Sivasagar E’POP inventory (SRC-005) and the Majuli E’pob inventory (SRC-084). This is a nomenclatural review, not a specimen identification.

## Question

Can SRC-084's *Acmella paniculata* be merged with SRC-005's source-reported *Spilanthes acmella* Murr. as the same taxon?

## Evidence reviewed

1. Kew Plants of the World Online (POWO), *Acmella paniculata*: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:914780-1  
   POWO accepts *Acmella paniculata* (Wall. ex DC.) R.K.Jansen and lists *Spilanthes paniculata* Wall. ex DC. and *Spilanthes acmella* var. *paniculata* (Wall. ex DC.) C.B.Clarke among its synonyms.
2. POWO, *Spilanthes acmella* (L.) L.: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:250748-1  
   POWO treats this name as a synonym of *Blainvillea acmella* (L.) Philipson under its current taxonomic backbone.
3. Singh et al., “Notes on three new immigrant species of *Spilanthes* Jacq. (Asteraceae) in India and the identity of the common ‘tooth-ache plant’,” accessible via PubMed Central: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3331556/  
   The article warns that Indian materials historically identified under the label “*Spilanthes acmella* Murr.” may be a mixture of species, and discusses *Spilanthes paniculata* as the correct name for material often referred to by that misapplied name in the literature.

## Decision

**Do not merge the inventory entries.** The author citation and taxonomic concept used in SRC-005 (“*Spilanthes acmella* Murr.”) are not demonstrated to be equivalent to the identified SRC-084 entry (*Acmella paniculata* (Wall. ex DC.) R.K.Jansen). The botanical literature gives a concrete reason to be cautious: the legacy label can be misapplied and may cover mixed material.

This does not prove that the two starter ingredients are different plants. It means the accessible literature is insufficient to decide whether they are the same taxon in these two field studies.

## Data handling

- Keep both source-reported taxon strings intact.
- Keep the candidate overlap excluded from the count of 14 exact-name overlaps.
- Do not alter the 31-row SRC-005 transcription or 48-row SRC-084 transcription based only on nomenclatural inference.
- Seek the original botanical authority, voucher/accession number, specimen image, or author clarification for each paper. If no voucher exists, mark the identification unresolved rather than manufacturing certainty.
- Keep plant-part fields separate; even confirmed taxonomic identity would not make the reported uses/parts interchangeable.

## Consequence for Assam coverage

ASM-GAP-045 remains **Open**. This follow-up strengthens the rationale for retaining the taxonomic flag, but it does not increase the confirmed overlap count, close the gap, or justify a new beverage or locality record.
