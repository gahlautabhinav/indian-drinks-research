## Draft email

**Subject:** Clarification request on voucher records and quantitative metrics in your 2026 Discover Food article

Dear Dr Talukdar,

I am conducting a source-verification audit of your article, “Cultural ecology and conservation of traditional beverage practices among selected communities of Assam, Northeast India” (*Discover Food*, 2026; https://doi.org/10.1007/s44187-026-01101-w), for a research archive focused on preserving source-faithful records and making uncertainty explicit.

Would you be able to clarify a few reproducibility points, or direct me to the appropriate co-author or herbarium contact?

1. **Voucher identifiers:** Table 1 prints ST/EES/2024/015 for both *Drymaria diandra* (row 1) and *Pteridium aquilinum* (row 10). Could you confirm whether this is intentional, a publication typo, or two distinct specimen/accession records? If available and appropriate to share, accession numbers or images of the relevant specimen labels would help verify the records.
2. **Life-form counts:** The Results text lists 17 herbs, 13 shrubs, 9 trees, 4 climbers and 4 ferns (sum 47), whereas the paper reports 41 species and Table 1 has 41 entries with labels including “small tree” and “woody climber.” Could you confirm the intended category counts and how the categories were grouped?
3. **Fidelity Level (FL):** Methods define FL (%) as (Ip/Iu) × 100, while reported values include 3.78 and 3.25 and are described as high. Could you clarify the intended formula, definitions of Ip and Iu, whether the displayed values are percentages or another scale, and the numerator/denominator counts used?
4. **CKSI reproducibility:** If feasible, could you point me to the formula and inputs or calculation sheet used for the reported CKSI values, so they can be interpreted without guessing?

I will retain the published values as printed and will not infer specimen identities or recalculate the indices without supporting information. Any clarification you can provide will be attributed appropriately in the archive. I am not seeking private participant details or any knowledge that should remain restricted.

Thank you for your time and for documenting these beverage-related plant traditions.

Kind regards,
