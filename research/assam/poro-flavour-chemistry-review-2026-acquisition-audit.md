**Candidate item:** Chiranjib Mili, Manab Jyoti Lagachu, Jwngma Basumatary and Kumananda Tayung, “Decoding the flavour chemistry of PO:RO APONG, a traditional rice beer of Northeast India,” *Food Research International: Reports*, article 100006, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frinr.2026.100006.

## What the publisher abstract says

The abstract frames PO:RO APONG flavour development as an interaction among:
1. phytochemicals from plants used in the OPOB / starter culture;
2. fermentative microbial communities; and
3. ash derived from rice straw/husk.

It highlights possible roles for Maillard reactions and ash-mediated flavour chemistry and discusses secondary metabolites that may contribute to flavour/sensory properties. The publisher record describes it as the first review specifically attempting to synthesize PO:RO APONG flavour chemistry. The record reports 76 references and says data generated by the review are included in the manuscript and supplementary data.

These are **review-level claims and synthesis themes**, not proof that each mechanism or compound has been measured directly in PO:RO APONG. Until the full text is obtained, do not turn the abstract into a list of measured volatile compounds, plant metabolites, microbial pathways or sensory outcomes.
