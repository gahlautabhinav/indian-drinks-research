# SRC-124 supplementary-material retrieval audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-31  
**Parent source:** Talukdar et al. (2026), *Cultural ecology and conservation of traditional beverage practices among selected communities of Assam, Northeast India*, *Discover Food* 6, article 376.  
**Supplement inspected:** Supplementary Material S1, four-page PDF titled “Questionnaire Sheet”.

## Retrieval result

The publisher's article page exposes Supplementary Material S1. The PDF is an interview / PRA / focus-group questionnaire, not a respondent dataset, species inventory, village register, or beverage-by-beverage results table.

It contains 36 questions covering:
- respondent community, age, gender, occupation, village/district and years of beverage-preparation involvement;
- beverage names Jou, Choko/Chubitchi, Xajpani and other;
- rice/grain, plant species and plant parts used in starter culture;
- collection habitat and season, preservation, fermentation duration and vessel;
- cultural occasions, participants, collection/preparation taboos and offerings;
- plant availability, harvesting rules, substitutes and knowledge transmission;
- perceptions of health benefits and community benefit from commercialisation.

The questionnaire explicitly places “bakhor/vekur/angkur” together as starter-culture terminology in one question. This documents the researchers' questionnaire framing only; it does **not** demonstrate that the terms are synonyms or interchangeable within Bodo, Rabha and Ahom communities.

## What this does and does not support

1. The supplement confirms the study's interview domains and the categories researchers intended to ask about.
2. It does not reveal the actual village names, individual respondent answers, plant-by-community rows, plant-by-beverage assignments, or the underlying values for CKSI / FL.
3. The article's main HTML exposes the heading for Table 1 but not its rows. The supplement does not supply the missing plant table.
4. The broad multiple-choice fermentation-duration options in the questionnaire are not observed fermentation durations for any named beverage.
5. The questionnaire asks respondents whether they perceive health benefits; this is not clinical evidence of health effects.
6. No new beverage, locality, ingredient, process, or quantitative ethnobotanical value is added to canonical CSVs from this supplement.

## Research decision

Keep SRC-124 as comparative, sampled ethnobotanical evidence for Bodo (Kokrajhar/Baksa), Rabha (Goalpara/Kamrup) and Ahom (Tinsukia/Dibrugarh). Do not use the questionnaire to infer product-specific starter equivalence or locality attribution. The primary GI-specification gap for Bodo Jou applications 968–970 remains open; the supplement cannot close it.

**Next useful retrieval:** obtain the authors' underlying plant table / community-wise dataset or request clarification of the FL calculation and starter terminology. Until then, retain the existing source-quality flags and do not add unsupported canonical rows.
