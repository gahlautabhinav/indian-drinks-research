# SRC-220 — Charaideo district-formation notification retrieval audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-14  
**Scope:** Recover and inspect the primary Government of Assam district-formation order for Charaideo, previously inaccessible through the Inspector General of Registration / Directorate of Land Records archive listing.

## Retrieval result

Recovered the 34-page merged scan hosted by the Government of Assam General Administration Department:

- Landing page: https://gad.assam.gov.in/index.php/document-details/notification-issued-districts-creation-majuli-hojai-biswanath-west-karbi-anglong-charaideo-bajali
- PDF: https://gad.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/public_utility/btc%20dist%20creation_merged_11zon.pdf
- Relevant scan: PDF page 4 (zero-indexed page 3), headed Government of Assam, General Administration Department, Orders by the Governor, Notification, dated Dispur, 25 January 2016.
- Order number: **No. GAG (B) 27/2005/261**.
- Operative wording: the Governor sanctions formation of a new administrative district to be known as **Charaideo District**, comprising **the whole area of the existing Charaideo Civil Sub-Division of the District of Sivasagar**, with its headquarters at **Charaideo**.
- Signed by B. R. Samal, IAS, Commissioner & Secretary to the Government of Assam, General Administration Department.

This is a primary scanned government order and resolves the previously inconsistent notification identifier. The Charaideo district website's abbreviated reference “GAG(B) 27/01/2016” is not the order number shown on the recovered scan. NABARD's district PLP records independently give GAG(B)/27/2005/261 dated 25 January 2016.

## What the order establishes

1. Charaideo district formation was sanctioned on 25 January 2016.
2. The legal territorial unit described in the operative clause is the **entire existing Charaideo Civil Sub-Division of Sivasagar district**.
3. The district headquarters is stated as Charaideo.

## What it does not establish

- The scanned notification page does **not** enumerate individual revenue villages, gaon panchayats, mouzas, revenue circles, sample households or coordinates.
- It does not mention Bharalisuk or Konwergaon.
- It does not establish that the 2015 A1/A2 samples were collected within the Charaideo Civil Sub-Division.
- It does not resolve the discrepancy between current administrative pages over the treatment of Nazira (part), nor does it supply the historical subdivision boundary map or a village-level schedule.

The order therefore improves the legal boundary evidence but **does not close ASM-GAP-076**. A separate authoritative record defining the 2015 Charaideo Civil Sub-Division's village membership (or a contemporaneous subdivision map/record) and the original Handique field register remain necessary.

## Retrieval false-positive control

A separate Assam Legislative Assembly Digital Library item titled “Council of Ministers Notification 2016 The Assam Gazette Notifications” was opened and its two-page PDF inspected. It is an 18 January 2020 Parliamentary Affairs Department portfolio-allocation notification, not a district-formation order. It is excluded from the evidence chain.

## Archive decision

- Register the recovered GAD notification as **SRC-220**.
- Retain SRC-202 as the separate IGR/DLRS archive listing whose linked PDF had timed out; do not merge its identity with this recovered GAD-hosted scan.
- Update ASM-GAP-076 to record the exact legal order and its scope limitation.
- Keep A1 Bharalisuk and A2 Konwergaon unresolved; no Charaideo beverage-specific place row is justified by this administrative order.

## Addendum — circle-level successor-territory reconciliation (SRC-193, SRC-195, SRC-205, SRC-221)

The official 2011 Sivasagar DCHB Part B (SRC-193) states that the then district had three civil subdivisions — Sivasagar, Nazira and Charaideo — and six revenue circles: Dimow, Sivasagar, Amguri, Nazira, Sonari and Mahmora. The current Sivasagar district profile (SRC-221) lists only Sivasagar Sadar and Nazira Civil subdivisions and four circles: Sivasagar, Amguri, Demow and Nazira. Current Charaideo administrative sources SRC-195/SRC-205 list Sonari and Mahmora under Charaideo. Read together with SRC-220's order that transfers the entire Charaideo Civil Sub-Division, this provides a strong circle-level administrative reconciliation that Sonari and Mahmora are in the Charaideo successor territory.

This strengthens the administrative plausibility of the A2 candidate: SRC-193 identifies 2011 Census village code 293217, Konwar Gaon, under Mahmora, and the locality is now within Charaideo's Mahmora administrative geography. It does **not** prove that the 2015 paper's spelling “Konwergaon” denotes that exact village/code. The name-to-code alias remains unconfirmed; A1 Bharalisuk remains unmatched. The crosswalk is therefore stronger at the district/circle level but still insufficient for a beverage-specific place row.
