## 2. Critical distinction: application List of Producers vs Part B authorised users

The attached **GI List of Producers** is an application-stage document dated 16 January 2019. It must not be treated as equivalent to the later **Part B register** of authorised users.

The statutory route is distinct:
- Section 17 of the Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999 allows a person claiming to be a producer of registered GI goods to apply to be registered as an authorised user.
- Rule 56 sets out the application procedure (Form GI-3, statement of case and affidavit; the current rule text should be used for any formal filing).
- Rule 59 provides for entry of an authorised user in Part B after the prescribed process.

Official legal references:
- Act, Section 17: https://ipindia.gov.in/page-content/geographical-indications-act-1999
- Rules, Rule 56: https://ipindia.gov.in/acts/geographical-indications-rules/rule-56-3
- Rules, Rule 59: https://ipindia.gov.in/acts/geographical-indications-rules/rule-59-3

Accordingly, a person appearing in the original 2019 producer list is not automatically proven to be a currently registered Part B authorised user. Conversely, failure to find a name in an inaccessible current register is not evidence that the person never produced Judima.

## 3. Published 2023 Part B snapshot: Judima not in the paper's named list

Pangging et al. (2023), “Status of Geographical Indications in the Northeast Region of India,” *Journal of Intellectual Property Rights* 28(4), pp. 304–311, reports that nine GI goods in Northeast India had Part B authorised-user registration in the dataset reviewed by the authors. Its named list includes Idu Mishmi textile, Assam (orthodox), Boka Chaul, Muga silk, Joha rice, Chak Hao, Mizo chilli, Naga mirchi and Sikkim large cardamom. **Judima is not among those nine named goods.**

The paper separately identifies Judima as Class 33 (alcoholic beverages except beer), with DIMAJIK HOSOM as applicant and YADEM and Assam Agricultural University as facilitators.

Interpretation: this is a published 2023-era snapshot indicating that the paper did not count Judima among the nine GI goods with Part B registrations in its dataset. It is not a current 2026 authorised-user count, does not prove that no Judima producer has since registered, and does not replace the live Part B register.
