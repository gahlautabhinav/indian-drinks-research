# SRC-136 Epop inventory — cross-source comparison with Mising studies

**Audit date:** 2026-11-04  
**Sources compared:** SRC-136 (Dibrugarh, 2012), SRC-005 (Sivasagar, 2023), SRC-006 (Lakhimpur section, 2012), SRC-007 (Majuli, 2022).

## 1. Inventory sizes and scope

| Source | Reported inventory | Locality scope |
|---|---:|---|
| SRC-136 | 16 taxa | Mising household samples from Dibrugarh district; named villages not supplied |
| SRC-005 | 31 taxa | 15 Sivasagar villages surveyed; in-depth study at Majorbari and Ligiribari |
| SRC-006 | 12 taxa | Mising subsection explicitly undertaken in Lakhimpur district; no village-level allocation |
| SRC-007 | 21 taxa | Majuli; authors report visits to more than 20 villages/households but do not name them or allocate taxa by village |

These are source-specific inventories. They must not be summed as 80 unique plants or collapsed into one universal Epop recipe.

## 2. Preliminary string-level overlaps

A conservative normalized-text comparison of the published taxon strings (case and punctuation removed; no botanical synonym resolution) identifies:
- SRC-136 ↔ SRC-005: *Selaginella* sp. (genus-level entry only).
- SRC-136 ↔ SRC-006: *Scoparia dulcis* (source spelling retained).
- SRC-136 ↔ SRC-007: four matching source strings: “Hydrocotyl rotundifolia”, “Centala acciatica”, “Swertia chirata” and “Piper longum”.

This is a **string-level crosswalk**, not a final taxonomic identity determination. Several spellings in SRC-136 and SRC-007 are likely erroneous or inconsistent. The apparent overlap should be checked against vouchers, botanical authorities and original table images before treating entries as confirmed species-level matches.

## 3. What the preliminary comparison means

- There is some repeated plant reporting across different Mising district/island studies, but the exact overlap is limited under conservative string matching.
- The four SRC-136/SRC-007 overlaps are useful audit leads, not proof of identical local names, plant parts, or recipe function.
- A taxon appearing in an inventory does not prove that every household or village uses it.
- The 2012 SRC-136 article explicitly notes that the number of plants can vary from place to place within the Mising community.
- The 31-taxon SRC-005 study has its own scope and sampling design; it should remain distinct from the 16-taxon 2012 list.

## 4. Next reconciliation work

1. Visually verify the printed names in each source table and preserve each original spelling.
2. Map spelling variants to accepted taxonomic names only where botanical evidence supports the match.
3. Compare plant parts and local names independently; species-name overlap alone is insufficient.
4. Compare source-specific roles (starter ingredient, flavouring, reported medicinal use) without assuming equivalence.
5. Obtain voucher identifiers or author clarification wherever available.

**Conclusion:** The 16-entry SRC-136 inventory is now independently transcribed and compared with three major Mising Epop inventories. The comparison establishes a small preliminary overlap, but botanical synonym resolution and plant-part reconciliation remain open.
