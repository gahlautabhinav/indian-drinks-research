# SRC-095 screening note — 2026 systematic review

**Source:** Barman, P.; Roy, K.; Sahu, R. (2026), “Systematic review on traditional fermented rice beverages of Assam,” *Next Research* 4, article 101241. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nexres.2025.101241  
**Access date:** 2026-10-23  
**Review level:** Abstract and publisher preview only; full text not reviewed.

## What it contributes

The accessible abstract frames the review around Assam's traditional fermented rice beverages, including rice substrates, starter cakes, microbes, fermentation processes, bioactive compounds and traditional plant use. It is a useful discovery source for tracing primary papers and checking whether the archive has missed beverage/process literature.

## What it does not establish in this pass

- No district, village or producer map can be derived from the abstract.
- The underlying study methods, search databases, inclusion/exclusion criteria, beverage taxonomy and evidence quality have not yet been checked.
- The abstract's broad references to bioactive compounds or potential pharmacological/toxicological effects are not clinical evidence and should not be repeated as health claims without tracing the underlying primary studies.

## Archive decision

Register as **review-level discovery evidence only**. Do not add place rows, promote district coverage, or change a beverage's confidence level from the abstract alone. Next action: obtain and inspect the full text; extract its included-study table and references; compare those references against the archive's source register; add only genuinely missing primary sources and retain the review's own limitations.
