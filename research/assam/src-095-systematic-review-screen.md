# SRC-095 screening note — 2026 systematic review

**Source:** Barman, P.; Roy, K.; Sahu, R. (2026), “Systematic review on traditional fermented rice beverages of Assam,” *Next Research* 4, article 101241. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nexres.2025.101241  
**Screening date:** 2026-10-23  
**Access level:** Publisher search-indexed article preview and abstract were readable; direct publisher-page retrieval returned an access restriction, and ResearchGate explicitly reports no full text available. This is **not** a full-text review.

## What the accessible material establishes

- The review describes a PRISMA-guided selection process: 2,997 initial results; 512 duplicates removed; 2,204 excluded for relevance; 233 excluded by title/abstract; six excluded after full-text screening; and 42 fermented-beverage studies retained. These are the review authors’ reported counts; the inaccessible full methods and flow diagram have not been independently checked.
- The preview identifies rice substrate, starter cakes, microorganisms, fermentation, traditional plants, bioactive compounds, and cultural significance as major themes.
- The preview says the review includes a table on beverages and starter cakes across communities including Deori, Mising, Ahom, Dimasa, Bodo, Karbi, Rabha and Sonowal Kachari. The table itself was not available for row-by-row extraction in the material inspected.
- Search-indexed reference snippets expose several candidate primary or earlier review sources, including Das, Deka & Miyaji (2012), Bhuyan & Baishya (2013), Saikia et al. (2007), Tanti et al. (2010), Bora et al. (2016), Das et al. (2019), Deka & Sarma (2010), Chakrabarty et al. (2010/2014), and Kalita et al. (2021). This is a **partial discovery list**, not a verified extraction of all references or of the 42 included studies.
- The publisher search result advertises 166 references, while a ResearchGate page reports 153 references. The discrepancy may reflect different metadata/export coverage; it is unresolved and neither count is treated as the included-study count.

## Preliminary overlap with the archive

Several visible titles overlap sources already represented in the register, including:
- Das, Deka & Miyaji (2012), starter-culture methodology — see existing SRC-006 / SRC-014 records (check canonicalisation before citing).
- Bhuyan & Baishya (2013), ethnomedicinal plants used in traditional rice beer — SRC-018.
- Deka & Sarma (2010), Rabha starter herbs — SRC-017.
- Das et al. (2019), microbiota/metabolites of Assam rice-beer varieties — SRC-087.
- Bora et al. (2016), Xaj-pitha starter-culture metagenomics — search the register by exact title before creating any new source record.
- Kalita et al. (2021), traditional rice beer of Assam — search the register by exact title before treating it as missing.

The preview snippets are not sufficient to certify that these are all among the 42 included studies, nor to establish which source supplied any particular geographic or beverage claim. Exact-title overlap should be checked against the whole source register before new IDs are assigned; duplicate bibliographic records must not be added.

## What remains unverified

- Full search strings, databases, date ranges, eligibility criteria, risk-of-bias/evidence-quality procedures, and the complete PRISMA flow.
- The complete included-study list and the article’s beverage/starter table.
- Whether each referenced source was included in the systematic synthesis or only cited for background.
- The locality, community, and beverage attribution of the underlying primary studies.
- Any clinical or safety inference from the review’s discussion of medicinal plants, metabolites, or pharmacological potential.

## Archive decision

Keep SRC-095 as **review-level discovery evidence only**. Do not create place rows, promote district coverage, or raise beverage confidence on the strength of this review preview. Do not repeat the review’s pharmacological or health language as established benefit. The next high-value step is to obtain a lawful full-text copy or supplement from the authors/library, then transcribe the included-study table and PRISMA details and crosswalk each reference to existing source IDs before adding only demonstrably missing primary sources.


## Reference crosswalk extension — verified candidate sources

A follow-up exact-title search confirmed four relevant references visible in the publisher-indexed preview that were **not present as exact-title records in the source register at the time of screening**. They have now been registered as discovery sources:

| New ID | Reference | Evidence role | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| SRC-096 | Tanti et al. (2010), “Ethnobotany of starter cultures used in alcohol fermentation by a few ethnic tribes of Northeast India” | Multi-state primary ethnobotanical/starter-culture study; includes Assam community starter/beverage pairs | Exact village-level Assam mapping still needs table extraction; not a current production census |
| SRC-097 | Chakrabarty, Sharma & Tamang (2009), “Substrate utilisation in traditional fermentation technology practiced by tribes of North Cachar Hills district of Assam” | Primary study on substrates and locally named fermented products | Historical district label; product/village attribution needs full-text extraction |
| SRC-098 | Chakrabarty, Sharma & Tamang (2014), “Traditional technology and product characterization of some lesser-known ethnic fermented foods and beverages of North Cachar Hills District of Assam” | Primary documentation and product characterization of Judima and Humao starter among selected products | Not a complete district beverage census |
| SRC-099 | Kalita et al. (2021), “Traditional rice beer of Assam, North East India: traditionalism, ethnobiology and its pharmacomedicinal trends” | Secondary review useful for reference discovery | Trace claims to primary sources; do not use medicinal framing as clinical evidence |

These records were added because the titles and publication details could be checked against accessible journal/repository pages or article PDFs, not merely because they appeared in the review. Their registration does **not** imply that SRC-095's full included-study table was extracted or that all four were among the 42 included studies. The review's full reference list and inclusion status remain unresolved. Exact source records already present include SRC-023 (Bora et al. 2016, Xaj-pitha starter metagenomics), SRC-071 (Saikia et al. 2007, Tai-Ahom food/beverage ethnobotany), SRC-087 (Das et al. 2019, Assam rice-beer microbiota/metabolites), SRC-017 (Deka & Sarma 2010, Rabha starter herbs), and SRC-018 (Bhuyan & Baishya 2013, rice-beer plant review). Some older register entries for other reviews have duplicate aliases; consult their canonicalisation notes before citing them.
