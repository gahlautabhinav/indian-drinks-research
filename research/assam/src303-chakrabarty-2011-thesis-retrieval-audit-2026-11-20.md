# SRC-303 retrieval audit — 2011 North Cachar Hills fermented-foods thesis

**Review date:** 20 November 2026  
**Target:** *Microbiological and Nutritional Analysis of Some Fermented Foods Consumed by Different Tribes of North Cachar Hills District of Assam* (2011)  
**Related records:** SRC-097 (2009 paper), SRC-098 (2014 paper), SRC-109 (2017 Nduiyi/Nduijao paper), ASM-GAP-123

## Executive finding

The thesis is bibliographically corroborated by multiple later records, including an official academic CV, but the thesis itself was not recovered in this pass. It must therefore be treated as a high-priority acquisition target, not as a source from which beverage findings can yet be extracted.

## 1. Identity and citation variants

- Prof. G. D. Sharma's official academic CV lists **Jaysree Chakraborty**, the exact thesis title, year **2011**, and **Assam University** in its list of PhD students supervised.
- Chakrabarty (2017), the Nduiyi/Nduijao article, cites **Jayasree Chakrabarty (2011)** with the same title and identifies Assam University, Silchar.
- Tamang et al. (2012), in its bibliography, cites the same title/year but gives the institutional wording as **Food Microbiology Laboratory, Sikkim Government College, Gangtok (Assam University)**.
- A 2022 review cites the thesis as a Shodhganga thesis, but the accessible citation supplies no Shodhganga handle or direct thesis URL.

These references strongly indicate the same thesis, but the author-name variants (Jaysree/Jayasree; Chakraborty/Chakrabarty) and institutional wording should not be silently normalised. A thesis title page or authoritative catalogue record is needed to settle the canonical form.

## 2. Retrieval attempts and current status

Searches conducted on 20 November 2026:
- Exact thesis title.
- Author-name variants combined with the title and Assam University.
- Targeted Shodhganga/INFLIBNET queries.
- Targeted Assam University domain queries.

The searches returned citations and an official academic CV, but no direct thesis catalogue entry, handle, downloadable thesis PDF or full-text copy. This is a bounded search result, not proof that the thesis is absent from Shodhganga or the university library.

## 3. Related published work — keep source boundaries

- **SRC-097 (2009):** “Substrate utilisation in traditional fermentation technology practiced by tribes of North Cachar Hills district of Assam.” This is a separate published paper and reports beverage names including Judima, Zunak and Dekujao at a district-level scope in the existing archive. It does not replace the thesis.
- **SRC-098 (2014):** “Traditional technology and product characterization of some lesser-known ethnic fermented foods and beverages of North Cachar Hills District of Assam.” The recovered full-text PDF identifies the selected beverage as **Judima** and the non-food mixed amylolytic starter as **Humao**, alongside bamboo-shoot and meat products. Its abstract reports observation/interviews and 21 samples across five selected products, with microbial and nutritional analysis. It does not establish that the thesis contains the same sample set or that its findings can be merged with the 2014 paper.
- **SRC-109 (2017):** “Identification of Yeast Strain Isolated from Nduiyi of Dima Hasao District of Assam, India.” It reports Nduiyi as a Jeme Naga starter for Nduijao and cites the 2011 thesis. The 2017 paper's own full-text findings remain the evidence for the Nduijao record; do not backfill its methods or localities from the unrecovered thesis.
- **SRC-110 (2024):** Zao/Zou and Ndhuihi are separately recorded and should not be merged with Nduijao/Nduiyi based on spelling similarity.

## 4. What is not yet known

No thesis-specific claim is made about:
- Whether Nduiyi/Nduijao, Judima/Humao, Zunak, Dekujao or any other beverage is covered in the thesis chapters.
- The number of beverages, communities, samples, villages or households studied.
- Exact fieldwork dates, sampling design, fermentation recipes, plant ingredients, starter taxa, microbial isolates or biochemical/nutritional values.
- Whether the 2009, 2014 and 2017 publications reproduce thesis data, use overlapping samples, or represent separate fieldwork.
- Appendices, original field schedules, sample codes, photographs, vouchers or laboratory protocols.

## 5. Acquisition plan

1. Ask Assam University Central Library for the thesis catalogue entry, accession number, title page and availability of a digital or physical copy.
2. Search Shodhganga using both author-name variants and the supervisor's name; request the handle if indexed.
3. Contact the author and/or supervisors for an authorized copy and appendices, clarifying the author-name spelling and institutional affiliation.
4. Once obtained, inspect the title page, abstract, contents, methods, beverage chapters, tables, appendices and sample schedules before making any data changes.
5. Build a publication-to-thesis overlap matrix for SRC-097, SRC-098 and SRC-109 to prevent double-counting of samples or field observations.

## 6. Archive decision

- Added SRC-303 as a bibliographic acquisition lead only.
- Added ASM-GAP-123 (High/Open) for thesis recovery and content verification.
- No new beverage, locality, method, ingredient, sample or microbial records were created.
- Existing SRC-097, SRC-098 and SRC-109 remain separate published sources; no overlap is presumed.

**Status:** Thesis confirmed bibliographically; full text not recovered; no thesis-derived beverage evidence admitted to the archive.
