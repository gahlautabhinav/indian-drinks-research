## 1. SRC-224 — Handique & Deka (2016), earlier nine-village field survey

Full text: https://www.allsubjectjournal.com/assets/archives/2016/vol3issue1/3-1-88.2.pdf

The article, *Methodology of rice beer preparation by some ethnic communities residing in Sivasagar District of Assam, India: A survey*, reports a field survey conducted in **nine villages** from **November 2013 to February 2014**. It says the villages were densely populated by Ahom, Mising and Deori communities and that approximately 20–30 people from each community, mostly women, were interviewed. The researchers observed starter-culture and beverage preparation and collected plant samples with local assistance.

The acknowledgements identify seven key informants:
- Mrs. Putoli Konwer
- Mrs. Promila Bharali
- Mrs. Budhbori Gogoi
- Mr. Himanjal Deori
- Mrs. Rukmini Mili
- Mrs. Nirmala Deori
- Mrs. Dipali Mili

### What this changes

This is a useful earlier field-network lead. The names **Putoli Konwer** and **Promila Bharali** may be relevant to follow-up retrieval because the 2017 sample names include “Konwergaon” and “Bharalisuk”. However, the article does not name any of its nine villages, does not map an informant to a village, and does not link any informant to the later October 2015 A1/A2 sample collection. A surname/name resemblance is not locality evidence.

The article's 2013–2014 field survey is a separate study from the six-site October 2015 sample collection in SRC-011. Do not merge the two field schedules or infer that the 2016 paper's nine villages contain the six 2015 sample sites.
