# SRC-345/SRC-346 — Judima GI registration record and 2014 field-study voucher audit

**Audit date:** 25 November 2026

## SRC-345 — Gogoi (2016), Judima as a proposed GI

**Citation:** Jupi Gogoi (2016), “‘Judima’ The Traditional Rice Wine of Dimasa Community of Assam: A Potential Candidate for GI Registration,” *Journal of Intellectual Property Rights* 21(4), pp. 238–242.  
**Full text:** https://nopr.niscpr.res.in/bitstream/123456789/35371/1/JIPR%2021%284%29%20238-242.pdf

### What this paper establishes

This is a pre-registration legal and geographical-indication case study, not the actual GI Statement of Case or final production specification. It documents a proposed GI narrative and a source-attributed process:
- Humao starter cake from dried, chopped and powdered Thembra bark identified as *Acacia pennata*, mixed with rice flour and water, dough-shaped and dried 3–5 days.
- Glutinous Bora rice; the paper says Bairing is a preferred Bora rice variety.
- Rice boiled, cooled on bamboo mats, mixed with crushed starter and kept in earthen vessels.
- Fermentation 3–5 days depending on season; liquid strained for drinking.
- It says “Ju” in Dimasa means alcoholic beverages and “Dima” refers to Dimasa people, giving an etymological explanation of Judima.

### Critical status distinction

The article was published in July 2016 when Judima was only being discussed as a potential GI. It must not be cited as proof of the final registered GI specification or the final notified production area. Its description is a historical pre-registration source. The article itself discusses Dima Hasao as the territory for the GI application and proposes that the GI specification should identify rice varieties, Thembra, environmental conditions, processing conditions and a producer inspection system.

## SRC-138 addendum — Arjun et al. (2014): voucher and plant evidence

The 2014 paper reports a field survey in North Cachar Hills and collection of four plant species from different locations in that district for starter-culture research:
1. *Piper betle* — leaves
2. *Buddleja asiatica* — leaves (and article text also refers to twigs in use)
3. *Hedyotis scandens* — leaves/twigs
4. *Acacia pennata* — leaves and bark (paper's figure and table vary in plant-part detail)

The paper says the plant materials were taxonomically identified and authenticated by Dr Jashodeb Arjun and herbarium voucher specimens JA-1, JA-2, JA-3 and JA-4 were deposited in the Department of Zoology, Lumding College. It does **not** map each voucher code to a taxon in the accessible prose/table extraction, give accession numbers, or name the specific collection localities. The deposit statement is a departmental voucher deposit claim; it does not identify an independent herbarium accession or current custody.

The paper's Table 1 is a qualitative phytochemical screening of the four plant materials. Table 2 reports a Judima sample pH 5.1, ethanol 16 ± 2% v/v, carbohydrate 32.43 ± 2.7 mg/ml, protein 0.97 ± 0.18 mg/ml, free amino acids 3.21 ± 0.21 mg/ml and microbial counts at starter, day 1, day 3, day 5 and ripe/filtered stages. The article does not describe a microbial enumeration protocol in enough detail to assess the counts; it also does not identify the number of independent producer batches or map the tested drink to a named village/household.

### Method divergence: 2014 study versus 2016 GI discussion

Arjun et al. describe *Acacia pennata* bark plus other plant leaves/twigs in the starter cake, 5 kg rice per one large Humao cake, and 5–7 days before yellowish juice appears. Gogoi (2016) describes Thembra bark + rice flour starter dried 3–5 days, Bora/Bairing rice, earthen-vessel fermentation 3–5 days. These are source-specific descriptions and not a single universal specification. The 2014 paper's abstract also says 4–5 days, while its detailed method says 5–7 days. Preserve the internal variation.

## Archive decisions

1. Register Gogoi (2016) as SRC-345, explicitly pre-registration and secondary.
2. Register the official IP India Application 643 live registry record as SRC-346.
3. Update SRC-138's voucher interpretation: JA-1–JA-4 are reported plant vouchers, but taxa-to-code mapping and present custody remain unresolved.
4. Keep the four plant taxa as source-reported starter-associated plants; do not infer they all occur in every Humao cake or every Judima recipe.
5. Keep Arjun et al.'s 16 ± 2% as a source-attributed laboratory result only.
6. Do not treat Gogoi (2016) as the current GI specification. The registered Statement of Case, GI Journal copy and map must be retrieved directly to determine the legal specification and production area.
7. Keep the applicant-name discrepancy (YADEM in secondary sources vs “DIMAJIK HOSOM” in current IP India display) unresolved pending official historical/transfer records.
8. Do not create new village-level production links from the study's vague “different locations” in North Cachar Hills.

## Correction / follow-on evidence — 25 November 2026

The earlier note that secondary sources named YADEM while the live IP India page displayed DIMAJIK HOSOM as a possible applicant-name discrepancy was too cautious in one respect and incomplete in another. New sources SRC-347 and SRC-348 explain the roles:
- The Hindu interview with YADEM managing trustee Uttam Bathari says YADEM initiated the proposal in 2018; a Dimasa women's group called Dimajik Hosom was formed after advice to apply through a women's group; the 2019 application was made along with YADEM.
- Pangging et al. (2023), Table 1, lists DIMAJIK HOSOM as the applicant and YADEM plus Assam Agricultural University as facilitators.
- APEDA's YADEM “Registered Proprietor” label and IP India's “Applicant Name” field for DIMAJIK HOSOM are not identical field labels. The source trail therefore supports different applicant-group/facilitator/representative roles rather than a demonstrated proprietor transfer or contradiction.
- The official IP India page explicitly lists the List of Producers, Map and Statement of Case as application documents dated 16 January 2019. The actual contents have still not been retrieved; only their titles and dates are verified.

See research/assam/src347-348-judima-gi-applicant-facilitator-and-producer-count-audit-2026-11-25.md for the expanded evidence assessment. Do not infer the names or number of producers from the existence of the list document. The CTIL report's approximate 200 women involved is a stakeholder-reported scale estimate, not a count of current authorised users or the 2019 annexure.


## Further correction — APEDA applicant field and unresolved metadata (25 November 2026)

The earlier follow-on paragraph that treated APEDA's YADEM label as “Registered Proprietor” is incorrect. Direct inspection of APEDA's 2022 catalogue (SRC-350; foreword dated 21 April 2022, page 60) shows the field is labelled **Applicant Name** and reads “Managing Trustee, Youth Association for Development & Empowerment (YADEM).” The live IP India page currently labels DIMAJIK HOSOM as Applicant Name. Pangging et al. (2023) also lists DIMAJIK HOSOM as applicant and YADEM/Assam Agricultural University as facilitators, while The Hindu interview describes the 2019 application as made along with YADEM. This suggests collaborative application history but does not fully reconcile the difference in official/secondary metadata. Keep the applicant-name variation unresolved pending original Form GI-1, affidavit, examination report/reply and any amendment record. Do not describe APEDA as using “Registered Proprietor” or infer a proprietor transfer.

The registry document inventory includes original application materials, including the List of Producers, Map, Statement of Case, Form GI-1, affidavit, examination report and replies. The accessible document controls resolve to a generic PDF icon, not the underlying PDFs. The substantive contents remain uninspected.
