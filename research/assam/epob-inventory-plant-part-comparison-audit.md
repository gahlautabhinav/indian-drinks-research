# E’pob inventory crosswalk: plant-part comparison audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-24  
**Research gap:** ASM-GAP-045  
**Sources:** SRC-005 (Mili & Sundriyal 2023, Sivasagar) and SRC-084 (Borah 2026, Majuli)

## Purpose

The 48-entry Majuli crosswalk contains 14 exact scientific-name candidate overlaps with the 31-entry Sivasagar table. This pass checks whether a matching name also means the same plant part was reported. It does not establish that the same recipe, preparation practice, plant specimen, or community-wide tradition is shared.

SRC-005 describes fieldwork in 15 Sivasagar villages and in-depth study in Majorbari and Ligiribari. SRC-084 reports three Majuli villages, which remain unnamed in the accessible article text. Therefore, this is a study-to-study comparison, not a village-to-village comparison.

## Results

Across the 14 exact-name candidates, the existing crosswalk notes indicate **7 broad plant-part matches and 7 differences**:

The comparison preserves the source-level distinctions rather than collapsing “whole plant,” “leaf,” “young shoot,” “flowers,” and “stem” into a generic “plant material” category.

## Interpretation

1. **Exact taxon-name overlap is not equivalent to recipe identity.** The same reported scientific name can be used with different parts in the two inventories.
2. **The seven differences are ethnobotanically material.** For example, *Piper longum* is listed for fruit in Majuli but leaf in Sivasagar; *Saccharum officinarum* is stem in Majuli but leaf in Sivasagar. Those should remain distinct source records.
3. **“Broad match” is deliberately conservative.** Leaf/leaves is a wording normalization; “foliar part” for *Dryopteris filix-mas* is similar but not identical phrasing. It is not voucher-level verification.
4. **Counts are provisional to the transcription.** The original tables and any taxonomic/voucher corrections still need review. No plant-part fields or taxon names were overwritten by this audit.
5. **No temporal inference.** The 31 vs 48 inventory counts and RFC/UV values cannot be treated as a trend or directly compared without aligned methods and denominators.

## Data action

No canonical ingredient or beverage records added or deleted. The current crosswalk and raw source values remain authoritative for this comparison. Next priority is to verify the seven apparent part differences against the typeset source tables and to obtain SRC-084's village names and voucher information from the author if possible.
