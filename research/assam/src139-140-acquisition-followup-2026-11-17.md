**Research date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Reassess the acquisition route for Kardong's two 2011 Epop papers and identify a direct institutional route. No experimental results are inferred from titles or related papers.

## 2. Journal-title/abbreviation clarification

The Dibrugarh bibliography prints the journal name as *Journal of Assam Science Society*, volume 52, issue 2 (2011). An All India Peoples Science Network profile of the Assam Science Society states that the research journal was “renamed as JASS from the 2nd Issue of the year 2011.” This provides a plausible explanation for the “JASS” abbreviation used in later references, but does not independently identify the exact cover title or contents of volume 52, issue 2. Retain the Dibrugarh bibliographic transcription and record the naming note separately; do not silently substitute a different journal title.

Source: https://aipsn.net/2017/09/11/assam-science-society/

The Assam Science Society's current public downloads page exposes general society documents but no historic journal archive/back issues in the visible list:
https://assamsciencesociety.in/information/download

## 5. Acquisition request should ask for exact materials

A document request should ask for:
1. Kardong (2011), *Journal of Assam Science Society* 52(2), pp. 1–6, including issue cover and contents page.
2. Kardong, Sood, Yadav & Bora (2011), *Bulletin of Life Sciences* Vol. XVI, including its exact page range and complete article.
3. Devid Kardong's 2011 PhD thesis, preserving the title as printed in the CSIR–NEIST award listing: “Studies on Microbiological and Biochemical aspects of Chaimod(PO:RO): A Traditional Alcoholic Beverage of Mishing Tribes of Assam.”
4. The complete technical annexures, isolate register and raw tables for UGC project F. No. 32-617/2006(SR).

For SRC-139, specifically request the full methods and every fermentation timepoint table, with original units, replicate counts, temperature, starter dose, rice substrate and fermentation endpoint definition. For SRC-140, request strain/isolate codes, taxonomic identifications, amylolytic assay method, controls, units and numerical results.
