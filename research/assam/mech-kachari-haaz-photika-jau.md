# Mech Kachari beverage names: Haaz, Photika and Jau — Assam research note

**Status:** Provisional research lead; no canonical beverage entries created.
**Community label in the lead source:** Mech Kachari.
**Geographic confidence:** District-level regional leads only; no named village or confirmed current production locality.
**Key caution:** Do not assume the names Haaz, Photika and Jau are synonyms, or that Haaz pitha, Zu and Bakhar are starter names paired one-to-one with those products.

## What the sources actually say

### 1. Secondary review lists the names, but does not resolve pairings

Bhattacharya and Deka's 2023 review lists Mech Kachari with starter-culture terms **Haaz pitha, Zu and Bakhar**, beverage names **Haaz, Photika and Jau**, and the districts **Udalguri, Kokrajhar and Goalpara** as major inhabited districts. The table cites earlier works, but the reviewed table does not specify which starter is used for which beverage, whether the three beverage names describe distinct processes, or which district contains which product. The district list is not village-level evidence of production. [SRC-062](https://www.foodandnutritionjournal.org/volume11number2/biochemical-indices-and-consumption-pattern-of-traditional-alcoholic-beverages-by-tribal-communities-of-north-east-india-a-review/)

### 2. A separate source documents Jau/Jumai and Photika among Deoris

Dutta's 2014 socio-cultural account describes **Jau/Jumai** as a Deori rice beer and **Photika** as a distilled liquor prepared from Jau/Jumai. It also reports social hospitality and ritual offerings in a Deori context. The passage names no village and has limited methodological detail, so it is a cross-check rather than a definitive community-exclusive classification. However, it raises a real attribution question: a later review lists Jau and Photika under Mech Kachari too. This could reflect shared regional terminology, overlapping use, citation carry-over or a review-table error; current evidence does not settle which. [SRC-063](https://globusedujournal.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/JJ14palash.pdf)

### 3. What is not established

- Whether **Haaz** is one product, an umbrella label, or a name used across more than one community.
- Whether **Photika** is consistently a distilled product in the Mech Kachari context, as it is described in the Deori account.
- Whether **Jau** among Mech Kachari refers to the same product/process as Deori Jau/Jumai.
- Which of **Haaz pitha, Zu and Bakhar** is used with each finished beverage.
- Which villages currently make each product, and whether the district list in the review describes community residence, source study areas, or beverage-production sites.
- Community-preferred spellings and meanings in the relevant language(s).

## Research decision

Keep this as a **naming-and-attribution investigation**, not a new canonical beverage record. Do not import the Deori preparation description into a Mech Kachari record or assign the three listed districts as confirmed production sites. First trace references 33 and 56 in the 2023 review to their original sources; then seek Mech Kachari-authored or community-verified sources and locality-specific interviews.

### Citation-trail audit: the review's references 33 and 56

The 2023 review's Mech Kachari row cites its references **33** and **56**. Reference 33 is Sarma (2017), *Rice Beer and Tribes of Assam*; it is a broad review/article with district-level discussion, including a general passage on Mech/Jau, rather than a village-specific production survey. Reference 56 is Senapati and Gurumayum (2016), *Medicinal Plants Used in Traditional Alcoholic Beverage Preparation by Tribes of Assam*. Its abstract names Dimasa, Boro, Rabha, Mising, Karbi, Ahom, Deori and Sonowal Kachari, but does **not** name Mech Kachari. Therefore reference 56 does not independently substantiate the Mech Kachari row on the evidence checked so far; the citation may be broad, indirect or mismatched. [SRC-054](https://www.iosrjournals.org/iosr-jhss/papers/Vol.%2022%20Issue7/Version-2/C2207021316.pdf) [SRC-064](https://rjpbcs.com/pdf/2016_7%285%29/%5B128%5D.pdf)

The table's district list is explicitly headed “major inhabited districts” for each community, not a list of confirmed villages producing each named beverage. Do not convert Udalguri, Kokrajhar and Goalpara into village-level production claims. The current evidence remains a **regional naming lead**, not a verified Mech Kachari product specification.
