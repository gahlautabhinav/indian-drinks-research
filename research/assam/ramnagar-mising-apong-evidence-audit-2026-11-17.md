# Ramnagar Mising / Charaideo Apong evidence audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Mahmora Ramnagar Mising Gaon / Ramnagar Mising Village, Charaideo; relationship of local Mising beverage evidence to the 2014 Jadovananda Taye chapter and the 2023 SRC-275 ÉPOP study.  
**Outcome:** No new beverage-specific place row and no starter-plant allocation to Ramnagar.

## 1. What is established

- SRC-282 is a verified bibliographic lead for Jadovananda Taye's 2014 chapter, *Religious Beliefs and Practices Among the Mising of Sivasagar District with Special References to Ramnagar Village*, in *Mosaic: A Collection of Research Articles*, ISBN 978-93-82283-91-1. The author's Dibru College CV lists the chapter and identifies Ramnagar Village as his permanent locality in present-day Charaideo. The chapter text and page range have not been recovered.
- SRC-284 is a Dibru College publication listing for Taye's 2023 chapter explicitly titled *Education and Development: A Study on the Mising Women of Ram Nagar Village of Charaideo District, Assam*. This independently supports a current Charaideo Ram Nagar locality label, but is not beverage evidence.
- SRC-281 is a 20-page Scribd-hosted compilation containing several unrelated Sonari College student projects. Its Mising project cover/certificate identifies *Festivals of Mising Community*, study area Ramnagar Mising Village, Charaideo, fieldwork date 5 March 2023. A separate permission letter authorises a field trip to Mahmora Ramnagar Missing Gaon on that date. Accessible text exposes objectives and certificates, not substantive findings/results. Do not treat the whole compilation as a Ramnagar ethnography.
- SRC-285 (2018 Assam education listing) and SRC-286 (2024 Charaideo DLSA address occurrence) independently support a Ramnagar locality in current Charaideo. SRC-279 (2026 Charaideo District & Sessions Judge document) independently uses the locality string Balikhuti Gomotha, PO Rangoli, PS Moranhat, Charaideo, corroborating the Balikhuti spelling trail for SRC-276.
- SRC-275 (Mili & Sundriyal, 2023) acknowledges villagers of Gumutha, Ramnagar and Balikuthi of Charaideo for support during field visits. Full-text methods are decisive for interpreting this correctly: the formal ÉPOP survey covered 15 randomly selected villages in Sivasagar district; Majorbari and Ligiribari were the two intensive sample villages. The paper's study-area section, map and methods do not designate the three Charaideo acknowledgement villages as survey sites. Its 31-species ÉPOP inventory is not allocated to Gumutha, Ramnagar or Balikuthi. Its general discussion of Apong ritual use is community-level context, not a Ramnagar observation.
- SRC-287 (Sharma & Pegu, 2011) reports Mising Apong offerings in Dobur Uie across eight Assam districts, including historical Sibsagar, but publishes no village-level sampling frame and cannot be assigned to present-day Charaideo or Ramnagar.

## 2. Important evidence boundary

Do not combine these into a false chain:

1. Mising households exist in Ramnagar.
2. Apong is documented as a Mising cultural beverage in Assam.
3. A 2023 ÉPOP research team acknowledged Ramnagar villagers.
4. Therefore Ramnagar households produced Apong or supplied ÉPOP samples.

The conclusion in item 4 does not follow. The 2023 article's methods and sample design point to Sivasagar villages; acknowledgements do not identify the role of each Charaideo village or any beverage data collected there. The 2014 chapter title signals a promising religious-practices source, but its contents must be inspected before recording ritual use.

## 3. Retrieval results on 17 November 2026

- Public searches for the exact chapter title, author and ISBN found the author's institutional CV and the official Assam RUSA plan corroborating the *Mosaic* edition, but no scan, library catalogue item, chapter pagination or full text.
- The 2023 Sonari College project compilation is discoverable, but the accessible text does not reveal the report's findings or any Apong reference.
- No new source establishes Ramnagar Apong production, consumption, subtype (Nogin/Poro), household, producer, preparation, starter-culture knowledge or current prevalence.

## 4. Next actions

1. Request the 2014 *Mosaic* volume (ISBN 978-93-82283-91-1) from Dibru College IQAC/library and/or Kaustubh Prakashan; obtain Taye's exact chapter and pagination.
2. Inspect the chapter for explicit Apong terminology, ritual context, direct observations/interviews, dates, and whether the evidence is Ramnagar-specific or general Mising context.
3. Request the original Sonari College Mising student report/appendices for the 5 March 2023 Ramnagar fieldwork; do not infer findings from its title or objectives.
4. Ask SRC-275's corresponding author to clarify the purpose, dates and outputs of the Charaideo visits, and whether any Apong/ÉPOP observations, interviews, samples or plant records were collected at Ramnagar, Gumutha or Balikuthi.
5. Keep the locality spellings distinct pending formal crosswalk: Ramnagar Mising Village; Mahmora Ramnagar Missing Gaon; Ram Nagar Village; Balikhuti Gomotha; Balikhuti Gumotha; Gomotha Balikhuti F.V. Census code 293278.

## 5. Archive decision

- Keep ASM-GAP-120 **High / Open**.
- Do not add an Apong or ÉPOP place row for Ramnagar from SRC-275, SRC-281, SRC-282, SRC-284, SRC-285, SRC-286 or SRC-287 alone.
- Do not assign SRC-275's 31 starter taxa to Ramnagar, Gumutha or Balikuthi.
- No beverage-specific production claim is verified for Ramnagar in this audit.
