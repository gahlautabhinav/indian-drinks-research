# SRC-084 Majuli 2026 — conservative Mising Apong inventory crosswalk

**Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Anchor inventory:** Borah (2026), SRC-084; 48 table entries, three unnamed Majuli villages, 28 reported informants.  
**Transcription:** `majuli-48-plants-src084.csv`.

## 1. Source integrity

SRC-084 reports 48 taxa in 30 families. Its table is numbered 1–48. The article reports three randomly selected Mising-inhabited villages but does not name them in the accessible text; no village-level records are created. The plant list is described as E’pob/starter-cake ingredients, not as 48 plants used in every batch or in each Apong type.

## 3. Do not merge

- *Hydrocotyle sibthorpioides* and *Centella asiatica* are both separately listed in SRC-084.
- *Zanthoxylum nitidum* and *Zanthoxylum oxyphyllum* are separately listed in SRC-084.
- SRC-084 *Z. nitidum* is not SRC-145 *Z. hamiltonianum* or SRC-136 “Z. hemiltonianum”.
- “Tinospora Miers” is not a species-level identification.
- Genus-level or incomplete names remain unresolved; no species-level synonym is inferred.

## 4. Interpretation

1. Recurrence across separate studies suggests some repeatedly reported ingredients, but inventories differ in field area, year, respondents, plant-part reporting and taxonomic quality.
2. A repeated binomial does not prove identical vernacular identity, plant part, starter function, seasonality or household prevalence.
3. RFC and UV in SRC-084 are source-specific ethnobotanical indices, not biochemical measurements or clinical evidence.
4. Do not sum the 48 SRC-084 entries with the 16/17/31/37-entry lists from other studies as a unique statewide species count.
5. The separate process descriptions in SRC-084 (Nogin: no ash; Po:ro: ash and Ta:suk) are important and should remain separate from the distinct SRC-136 and SRC-145 process descriptions.
