# Kardong thesis and 2011 Epop papers — acquisition audit

**Research date:** 2026-11-10  
**Scope:** Locate the 2011 Dibrugarh University thesis and two journal papers already registered as SRC-139 and SRC-140. This is an access/provenance audit, not a reconstruction of missing experimental results.

## 1. Thesis identity confirmed; full text not located

A CSIR-NEIST institutional publication report lists Dr Devid Kardong's PhD as awarded in 2011 by Dibrugarh University, under Dr. T. C. Bora, with the title printed as “Studies on Microbiological and Biochemical aspects of Chaimod(PO:RO): A Traditional Alcoholic Beverage of Mishing Tribes of Assam.”

This confirms the thesis bibliographic identity and award year. It does not expose the thesis abstract, contents, dataset or PDF. Preserve “Chaimod” from this record; do not silently replace it with “Saimod”.

Exact-title and author searches in the available public web index did not locate a thesis record or downloadable full text. Dibrugarh University's own thesis discovery pages direct researchers to Shodhganga, IndCat, KrishiKosh and other repositories. These are acquisition routes, not evidence that the thesis is deposited in any one of them.

## 2. 2011 papers: institutional bibliography confirms citation, not contents

The Dibrugarh University research-publication volume lists:

- **SRC-139:** D. Kardong, “Studies on the Changes of certain Parameters during fermentation of Saimod using Epop: The Traditional Starter for Preparation of Alcoholic beverage by Mishing Tribe of Assam, India,” *Journal of Assam Science Society* 52(2):1–6, ISSN 0587-1921 (2011).
- **SRC-140:** Devid Kardong, Kaushal Sood, R. N. S. Yadav and T. C. Bora, “Amyolytic activity of mold isolated from Epop used by the Mising tribe of Assam, India in preparation of alcoholic Rice beer,” *Bulletin of Life Sciences*, Vol. XVI, ISSN 0971-5746 (2011).

The institutional record spells the second title “Amyolytic”; retain it as bibliographic transcription. Exact-title searches did not recover either full article. No parameters, timepoints, isolate names, enzyme values, assay methods or numerical results are added here.

## 4. Evidence boundary retained

- SRC-152 remains the only directly accessible Kardong project report from 2007–2009. Its available five-page version confirms three Epop samples from unnamed places, seven amylolytic isolates, six alcohol-fermenting isolates and candidate strain codes AY-3/amy-3, AY-1(19), AY-4 and FY-10. It is not a substitute for the thesis or either 2011 article.
- A superficially similar 2017 amylolytic-fungi paper is about Bodo *amou* and Deori *perok-kushi* starter cakes. It is not Mising Epop evidence and is excluded from SRC-139/SRC-140 interpretation.
- Until primary texts are obtained, no longitudinal fermentation curve, Mising mould taxonomy or enzyme-activity value can be extracted from SRC-139 or SRC-140.
- No new source IDs, village records, plant records or microbial taxa are created by this acquisition audit.
