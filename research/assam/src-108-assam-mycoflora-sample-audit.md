# SRC-108 — Assam mycoflora study audit

**Research date:** 2026-10-23

**Citation:** Saikia, B., Ali, M. S., Gogoi, S. H., & Nath, P. D. (2024). “Isolation and Characterization of the Mycofloral Diversity in Traditional Assamese Alcoholic Fermentation from India.” *Asian Journal of Dairy and Food Research*, 43(1), 104–110. https://doi.org/10.18805/ajdfr.DR-1829

## Findings
- The abstract lists seven districts sampled during 2019–2020: Jorhat, Sivasagar, Golaghat, Lakhimpur, Dhemaji, Majuli and Karbi Anglong.
- The introduction/methods later describes a survey in six districts and nine starter samples. This seven-versus-six district discrepancy is unresolved and should remain flagged rather than silently corrected.
- Named starter materials: “Xaaj pitha,” “Apong,” and “Suzen (mod pitha).” Accessible sample description does not provide a verified village-by-village or community-by-community mapping for all nine samples.
- Authors report 16 yeast isolates and two filamentous fungi. Identified organisms include *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*, *Saccharomycopsis fibuligera*, *Candida tropicalis*, *Aspergillus oryzae* and *A. niger*. *S. cerevisiae* was reported as dominant across the nine samples.
- Sequence accessions reported: MK110643 (*S. cerevisiae*), MK110642 (*S. fibuligera*) and MK110644 (*C. tropicalis*).

## Limitations and Atlas decisions
1. The district list is not a verified village-level production map; do not add village records from this paper alone.
2. The source-specific starter names should not be equated automatically with finished-drink names. “Apong” here does not establish a Nogin or Po:ro subtype.
3. Nine starter samples are not a statewide census and do not establish universal microbial composition.
4. The discussion speculates about possible consumer-health implications from isolating *C. tropicalis*, but the study is not a finished-product exposure assessment or clinical/outbreak investigation. Do not treat that inference as proof of general danger or general safety.
5. The paper does not establish household recipes, universal alcohol percentages, current prevalence, or clinical benefits.

## Next verification
Retrieve the sample/table metadata, reconcile the six-versus-seven-district discrepancy, and compare the results with SRC-106/SRC-107 and existing Assam starter-culture sources before adding beverage or place records.
