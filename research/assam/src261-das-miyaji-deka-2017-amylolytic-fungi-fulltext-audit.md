# SRC-261 full-text audit — Amylolytic fungi in starter cakes for rice beer production
**Review date:** 20 November 2026  
**Citation:** Das, Arup Jyoti; Miyaji, Tatsuro; Deka, Sankar Chandra (2017), Journal of General and Applied Microbiology 63(4):236–245. DOI: 10.2323/jgam.2016.11.004.  
**Related gap:** ASM-GAP-121

## Executive finding
The full text resolves the collection districts for the two starter cakes, which were not available in the abstract-level record: Bodo amou was collected in Kokrajhar district and Deori perok-kushi in Lakhimpur district. Three replicates per starter type were collected. No villages, household producers, collection dates or replicate-independence details are published.

## 1. Collection provenance
The Materials and Methods section states:
- **Amou:** collected from the Bodo community residing in Kokrajhar district, Assam.
- **Perok-kushi:** collected from the Deori community residing in Lakhimpur district, Assam.
- **Replicates:** three per starter type, placed in sterile bottles, labelled according to place of collection, transported to the laboratory under refrigerated conditions on the same day and stored at 4°C.

The paper does not disclose the place labels, village names, household/producer identities, collection dates or whether the three replicates represent independent households, independent batches, or analytical subsamples. Do not convert three replicates into three localities.

## 2. Identified strains and accessions
| Starter | Community | Fungus | Strain | NCBI accession | Initial mould count |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Amou | Bodo | *Amylomyces rouxii* | TU460 | KU500802 | 7.67 ± 0.26 log CFU/g |
| Perok-kushi | Deori | *Rhizopus oryzae* | TU465 | KU535588 | 7.58 ± 0.55 log CFU/g |

The article identifies the fungi using ITS-region sequencing and reports the accession numbers above. These accessions are sequence records for the isolates, not sample-location identifiers.

## 3. Laboratory performance reported in Table 2
| Strain | Starch degradation zone | Saccharification ratio | Glucoamylase activity | α-amylase activity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| *A. rouxii* TU460 | 39.67 ± 1.53 mm | 64.25 ± 3.50% | 14.92 ± 1.58 μmol/min | 7.02 ± 0.98 unit/mL |
| *R. oryzae* TU465 | 27.67 ± 1.53 mm | 5.26 ± 0.11% | 1.41 ± 0.26 μmol/min | 6.09 ± 0.74 unit/mL |

Values are reported as means of three replications followed by standard deviation. The paper also reports that LC-MS/MS analysis detected no mycotoxins produced by either tested strain under the described assay conditions. This must not be presented as a safety certification for traditional starter cakes or finished beverages.

## 4. Interpretation limits
- The district-to-community association is direct source evidence.
- Village and household provenance remains unavailable.
- Replicate count is not a count of producers, villages or independent field sites.
- The strain accession numbers identify sequences, not geographic provenance.
- Laboratory starch hydrolysis and enzyme assays do not quantify alcohol, sensory quality or safety of the finished traditional beverages.
- The study must not be used to assign amou or perok-kushi to all villages in either district.

## 5. Research action
Request the de-identified collection labels/register and replicate design from the corresponding author, Sankar Chandra Deka. Ask specifically for the village/locality attached to each of the three amou and three perok-kushi replicates, collection dates, and whether replicates represent independent households, batches or analytical repeats. No outreach has been sent.

## 6. Archive decision
SRC-261 is upgraded from abstract-level to full-text-audited evidence. ASM-GAP-121 is High/Open. The archive may retain Kokrajhar district for Bodo amou and Lakhimpur district for Deori perok-kushi as district-level collection provenance, but must not create village-level place rows until the underlying collection register or equivalent primary evidence is obtained.
