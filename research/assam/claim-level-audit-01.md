# Assam claim-level source audit — tranche 1

**Date:** 2026-10-22  
**Scope:** Claim-level review of nine beverage records using recent Mising and Bodo research and a recent Rabha/Ahom research lead. This is not a full audit of all 22 Assam beverage records.

## Evidence standard

A claim is considered supported only at the scope the source actually documents. District-level fieldwork does not establish village-level production; a review or abstract is not a substitute for the full methods/results; a GI applicant address is not proof of a production locality; and similar names are not merged without direct evidence.

## Geographic limits confirmed in the new sources

### SRC-081 — Bodo survey

Deka et al. (2025) names Amlaiguri, Gendrabili, “Jalpaiguri” and Banshigaon as survey localities around Kokrajhar headquarters, reports 50 interviewees, and describes fieldwork in April–July 2022 and October–November 2023. The article discusses several beverage methods but does not consistently allocate each subtype to each village.

- The four names support a survey-locality claim, not proof that every beverage was produced in every village.
- Preserve “Jalpaiguri” as printed until authors or a reliable gazetteer verify the locality.
- Do not present one observed method as a universal Bodo recipe.

Place records ASM-PL-061 through ASM-PL-064 now cite SRC-081 and retain this limitation.

### SRC-080 — Mising survey

Gogoi et al. (2026) reports a 10-village Jorhat district survey with 50 key informants and names both Po:ro apong and Noggin apong. The accessible abstract does not give the ten village names or allocate each beverage to particular villages. ASM-PL-065 now cites SRC-080 and asks for full-text extraction before product-to-village claims are made.

This strengthens district/community-level evidence but does not yet justify village-level geographic confidence.

### SRC-082 — Rabha and Ahom lead

The publisher abstract reports a 2026 study of selected Bodo, Rabha and Ahom beverage practices using interviews, focus groups, participant observation and plant identification. It is recorded as a full-text retrieval lead only. No specific alias, village, plant or recipe claim was upgraded from the abstract alone.
