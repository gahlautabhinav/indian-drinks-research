# Nduijao and Nduiyi — source dossier (SRC-109)

**Source:** Jayasree Chakrabarty (2017), “Identification of Yeast Strain Isolated from Nduiyi of Dima Hasao District of Assam, India,” *International Journal of Current Microbiology and Applied Sciences* 6(3):587–594. https://doi.org/10.20546/ijcmas.2017.603.068

## Identity
The paper describes Nduiyi as a traditional mixed amylolytic dough starter used by the Jeme Naga community of Dima Hasao to prepare Nduijao, a sweet-sour alcoholic beverage. Keep starter and finished beverage separate. This source does not establish synonymy with Judima, Zunak, Dekujao or other Dima Hasao drinks.

## Starter-making account
The paper reports sticky rice soaked for 9–10 hours, crushed with either bark of *Albizia myriophylla* or leaves of *Saccharum officinarum*, and mixed with 2–3% previously prepared Nduiyi. Water is added to form paste, shaped into flat rounded/oval cakes, held for 1–3 days at 25–30°C, sun-dried for 2–3 days and stored dry. This describes the starter cake, not a complete beverage-brewing protocol or safety guidance.

## Localities
The article names Lodi, Asalu, Nutan Gunjung, Boro Haflong and Lisong Basti as localities where rural women prepare Nduiyi. Its table lists Nduiyi samples from Haflong Bazaar and Lodi Basti, and Nduijao samples from Haflong Bazaar and “Laisong Basti.” The Lisong/Laisong spelling discrepancy is unresolved. Two samples per table location are not a distribution census.

## Laboratory findings and limits
- Four Nduiyi samples were examined; isolates were identified phenotypically as *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* (spelling varies in paper) and *Candida glabrata*.
- Nduijao samples are reported with pH 4.3 ± 0.1, titratable acidity 0.44 ± 0.01, and alcohol 4.0–6.0%. These are study-specific results, not universal product specifications or safety thresholds.
- Traditional health/tonic claims are not clinical evidence. The paper is not a current village distribution survey.

## Atlas decisions
Added provisional beverage record ASM-026-NDJ and place leads ASM-PL-094–ASM-PL-099. Haflong Bazaar and Lodi Basti are sample locations; Asalu, Nutan Gunjung and Boro Haflong are localities named for starter production. Preserve Lisong/Laisong as an unresolved spelling pair. Do not infer coordinates or comprehensive geographic coverage.

## Follow-up
Verify spelling and current practice through community-led or authoritative local sources; seek field notes if available; independently corroborate beverage characterization.
