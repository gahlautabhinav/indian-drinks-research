## Sampling and microbiological findings explicitly visible
- Three Epop starter-culture samples from different places were collected.
- Seven pure isolates (bacteria and fungi/yeast) with significant amylolytic property and six isolates with alcohol-fermenting property were reportedly isolated.
- Partial characterization was carried out for amylolytic isolates labelled AY-3 (amy-3), AY-1(19), AY-4 (Big cell), and FY-10.
- The report suggests AY-3 and AY-4 together with FY-10 might form a suitable consortium for solid-state Saimod/Po:ro fermentation. This is a proposed candidate consortium, not a validated production starter or strain-level identification.
- The short accessible report does not provide isolate species identities, sequence data, complete biochemical assay values, sample localities, sample collection dates or detailed protocols.
- Do not treat “six alcohol-fermenting isolates” as six identified yeasts: the report's wording and displayed summary are too limited to support taxonomic assignments.

## Relationship to the two 2011 bibliographic leads
Dibrugarh University's 2011–2015 research-publication inventory lists two distinct 2011 papers by Devid Kardong and collaborators:
1. Kardong, Sood, Yadav and Bora, “Amyolytic activity of mold isolated from Epop used by the Mising tribe of Assam, India in preparation of alcoholic Rice beer,” *Bulletin of Life Sciences*, Vol. XVI, ISSN 0971-5746 (2011).
2. D. Kardong, “Studies on the Changes of certain Parameters during fermentation of Saimod using Epop: The Traditional Starter for Preparation of Alcoholic beverage by Mishing Tribe of Assam, India,” *Journal of Assam Science Society* 52(2):1–6, ISSN 0587-1921 (2011).
The UGC report is thematically related to the first and possibly to the second, but it does not establish that either paper is a direct publication of this report. The report's project title concerns isolation and biochemical characterization of potential amylolytic and alcohol-producing strains; the second paper title concerns fermentation parameter changes. Keep all three records distinct until original articles are retrieved and compared.

## Cross-check against SRC-136 (Kardong et al. 2012)
The 2012 Po:ro paper reports a 16-plant Epop inventory and beverage preparation/biochemical findings. Its microbial summary includes amylolytic activity, but the report's AY/FY isolate labels cannot be mapped to the 2012 paper's microbial descriptions without the full technical report or original isolate tables. No organism identity or enzyme value should be inferred from the 2009 project summary.
