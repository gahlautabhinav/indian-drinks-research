# SRC-091 quantitative follow-up (2026-10-23)

Publisher Version of Record date: 4 September 2026. Primary source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44187-026-01101-w

The paper reports 41 species and life-form counts of 17 herbs, 13 shrubs, 9 trees, 4 climbers and 4 ferns. These sum to 47; category overlap may explain the discrepancy, but the article does not clarify it. Do not calculate life-form percentages until clarified.

The Methods define Fidelity Level as (Ip/Iu) x 100 and label it a percentage, but Results/Discussion describe reported values such as 3.78 and 3.25 as high consensus. Without underlying counts, preserve these as published and mark the scale/interpretation unresolved; do not silently rescale or rank species by consensus.

The paper's six district study sites are not a village-level production census. Ethnographic reports about plant use, taboos and conservation should remain attributed to the study and should not be converted into causal ecological or clinical claims.
