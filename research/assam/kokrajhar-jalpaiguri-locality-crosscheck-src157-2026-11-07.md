# Kokrajhar “Jalpaiguri” locality cross-check (SRC-157)

**Audit date:** 2026-11-07  
**Question:** Does the 2025 Bodo beverage-methodology paper's “Jalpaiguri (10 km east)” refer to a locality in Kokrajhar district, Assam, or to Jalpaiguri district in West Bengal?

## Evidence reviewed

1. **Deka, Basumatary & Deka (2025), Journal of Ethnic Foods 12:23, DOI 10.1186/s42779-025-00286-2 (SRC-066).** The methods section names Amlaiguri (25 km north), Gendrabili (5 km south), “Jalpaiguri” (10 km east) and Banshigaon (2 km west), with each distance measured relative to Kokrajhar district headquarters. It reports 50 interviewees and fieldwork in April–July 2022 and October–November 2023. The paper does not provide village coordinates or a village map that resolves the spelling.

2. **District Administration, Kokrajhar, Flood Contingency Plan 2025 (SRC-157).** In its Kokrajhar Revenue Circle table, under Nayekgaon Forest Range, the plan separately lists “Amlaiguri FV”, “Jalpaiguri”, “Amlaiguri” and other villages. This is independent administrative evidence that a locality called Jalpaiguri exists within Kokrajhar district, Assam. It is not the West Bengal district simply because the name matches.

## Finding

The name “Jalpaiguri” in the 2025 beverage paper is **geographically plausible as a Kokrajhar-district locality**. The official district plan independently confirms a Kokrajhar namesake. This materially weakens the earlier assumption that the printed name is likely a mistaken reference to West Bengal.

However, the match is **not fully proven**: the contingency plan supplies no coordinates or distance from Kokrajhar headquarters, and does not explicitly connect its listed Jalpaiguri to the paper's 10-km-east survey site. The authors' original field notes, coordinates or confirmation are still needed before a precise normalized village identifier is assigned.

The same plan also lists “Barshangaon”; this must **not** be silently equated with the paper's “Banshigaon”. Similar spelling alone is not identity evidence. “Gendrabili” in the paper is also not silently normalized to “Choto Gendrabili” in other administrative material.

## Archive action

- Retain the source spelling “Jalpaiguri” and Kokrajhar district as the paper's stated survey context.
- Upgrade the locality note from “no corroborating local administrative evidence” to “Kokrajhar-district namesake independently attested; exact survey-site match unresolved”.
- Add SRC-157 to the existing place lead ASM-PL-063 and district evidence list. Do not create a second place row.
- Do not add any new beverage-to-village attribution: the paper does not map each beverage subtype to each of the four survey villages.
- Keep ASM-GAP-028 **Open** pending author confirmation/coordinates. The geographic plausibility check does not close the gap.
