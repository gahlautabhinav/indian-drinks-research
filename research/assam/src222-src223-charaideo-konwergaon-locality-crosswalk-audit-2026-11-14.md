# SRC-222 / SRC-223 — Charaideo A2 locality crosswalk audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-14  
**Scope:** Test whether Handique et al. (2017; SRC-011) A2 “Konwergaon” can be identified with the current Charaideo Mahmora locality officially styled “Mahmora Konwar Gaon”, and separately retest A1 “Bharalisuk”.

## 1. New primary/current administrative evidence

### SRC-222 — Government of Assam, Charaideo district AAGY scheme page
Official page: https://charaideo.assam.gov.in/scheme-page/axom-adarxo-gram-yojana-aagy

The Charaideo District Administration page lists a village-specific scheme document titled **“Mahmora Konwar Gaon (Entry Point Activities)”** among Charaideo village projects. The page describes the Axom Adarxo Gram Yojana as a village-development scheme and states that it was introduced in 2018–19. This confirms that the district administration recognises a present-day Charaideo village/project locality under the exact compound name Mahmora Konwar Gaon.

Limit: the page does not display a Census code, coordinates, historic alias, or evidence that the 2015 sample site in SRC-011 was this village. A scheme title is not a formal alias concordance.

### SRC-223 — Office of the Chief Judicial Magistrate, Charaideo, 2025 recruitment admit card
Official PDF: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec053f647cadf56541fb9513cb63ec37/uploads/2025/08/2025081296.pdf

The official admit card gives a candidate address as **“Mahmora Konwar Gaon P/O- Bharali Pukhuri P/S-Kakotibari Dist: Charaideo”**. This directly corroborates the contemporary compound locality name and its postal/admin association with Bharali Pukhuri and Charaideo.

Limit: an individual postal address is not a Census village-code or historical alias record and does not identify the 2015 sample household.

## 2. Census-code and successor-district reconciliation

SRC-192/SRC-193 (2011 Sivasagar District Census Handbook) identify **Konwar Gaon**, Census location code **293217**, under sub-district **Mahmora (02075)**. The 2011 DCHB is source-era material: at that time Mahmora was within Sivasagar district.

SRC-220 is the primary Government of Assam order dated 25 January 2016, No. GAG (B) 27/2005/261. It creates Charaideo district from the whole existing Charaideo Civil Sub-Division of Sivasagar. SRC-193's 2011 administrative tables place Mahmora in that Charaideo subdivision. Current Charaideo official sources SRC-195 and SRC-205 list Mahmora under Charaideo; current Sivasagar profile SRC-221 no longer lists it.

This resolves an important chronology issue: **SRC-011's October 2015 sample date precedes the 25 January 2016 district-formation notification.** Therefore, the paper's use of “Sivasagar district” is not inconsistent with a sample collected in the Mahmora/Charaideo-subdivision territory. The earlier Mariani alternative remains inconsistent because Mariani was part of Jorhat district by 2015, and Kokilamukh also appears in the Jorhat 2011 DCHB (SRC-212).

## 3. A2 decision

Evidence chain:
1. SRC-011 records an Ahom Xajpani sample A2 at “Konwergaon”, collected October 2015.
2. SRC-193 records a village “Konwar Gaon”, code 293217, in Mahmora sub-district, then part of Sivasagar district's Charaideo Civil Sub-Division.
3. SRC-220 transfers the entire Charaideo Civil Sub-Division to the new Charaideo district in January 2016.
4. SRC-222 and SRC-223 independently confirm that “Mahmora Konwar Gaon” is a current Charaideo locality, with Bharali Pukhuri postal geography.

**Assessment: high-plausibility candidate; not a confirmed sample-site alias.** The spelling similarity (Konwergaon / Konwar Gaon), the exact Mahmora administrative unit, and the correct 2015 source-era district chronology make this the strongest A2 candidate. However, no source explicitly says that Handique et al.'s “Konwergaon” means Census village code 293217 or the current Mahmora Konwar Gaon. Keep ASM-GAP-076 open pending original sample register, author clarification, coordinates, or a formal historical alias/census-code crosswalk.

Do not add a Charaideo beverage-specific place row yet. The metal-profile article confirms a sample from the named source locality; until the alias is established, the sample cannot safely be attributed to the current named revenue village.

## 4. A1 “Bharalisuk” — exact-name search and false-friend control

Targeted searches for “Bharalisuk”, “Bharali Suk”, “Bharali Sukh”, and combinations with Sivasagar, Charaideo, Sonari and Mahmora did not recover an authoritative locality record matching the exact sample spelling.

The following nearby/current names are **not** alias proof:
- **Bharali Pukhuri** — current Charaideo GP/postal locality; SRC-179 lists the GP under Sonari (Mahmora).
- **Mahmora Konwar Gaon, P.O. Bharali Pukhuri** — SRC-223 gives a current address.
- **Bharali Pukhuri-A/B/C** — education clusters in official education datasets, not revenue villages.
- **Dulia Bharali Gaon** — a separate historical Teok/Jorhat locality (SRC-208/SRC-209).

A1 may be a transcription variant, a micro-locality, a household/local name, or a distinct village. None can be selected without the field register or author confirmation. Do not equate “Bharalisuk” with “Bharali Pukhuri” by shared initial wording.
