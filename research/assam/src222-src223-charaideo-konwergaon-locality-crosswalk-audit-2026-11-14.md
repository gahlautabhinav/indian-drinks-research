# SRC-222 / SRC-223 — Charaideo A2 locality crosswalk audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-14  
**Scope:** Test whether Handique et al. (2017; SRC-011) A2 “Konwergaon” can be identified with the current Charaideo Mahmora locality officially styled “Mahmora Konwar Gaon”, and separately retest A1 “Bharalisuk”.

## 1. New primary/current administrative evidence

### SRC-222 — Government of Assam, Charaideo district AAGY scheme page
Official page: https://charaideo.assam.gov.in/scheme-page/axom-adarxo-gram-yojana-aagy

The Charaideo District Administration page lists a village-specific scheme document titled **“Mahmora Konwar Gaon (Entry Point Activities)”** among Charaideo village projects. The page describes the Axom Adarxo Gram Yojana as a village-development scheme and states that it was introduced in 2018–19. This confirms that the district administration recognises a present-day Charaideo village/project locality under the exact compound name Mahmora Konwar Gaon.

Limit: the page does not display a Census code, coordinates, historic alias, or evidence that the 2015 sample site in SRC-011 was this village. A scheme title is not a formal alias concordance.

### SRC-223 — Office of the Chief Judicial Magistrate, Charaideo, 2025 recruitment admit card
Official PDF: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec053f647cadf56541fb9513cb63ec37/uploads/2025/08/2025081296.pdf

The official admit card gives a candidate address as **“Mahmora Konwar Gaon P/O- Bharali Pukhuri P/S-Kakotibari Dist: Charaideo”**. This directly corroborates the contemporary compound locality name and its postal/admin association with Bharali Pukhuri and Charaideo.

Limit: an individual postal address is not a Census village-code or historical alias record and does not identify the 2015 sample household.

## 2. Census-code and successor-district reconciliation

SRC-192/SRC-193 (2011 Sivasagar District Census Handbook) identify **Konwar Gaon**, Census location code **293217**, under sub-district **Mahmora (02075)**. The 2011 DCHB is source-era material: at that time Mahmora was within Sivasagar district.

SRC-220 is the primary Government of Assam order dated 25 January 2016, No. GAG (B) 27/2005/261. It creates Charaideo district from the whole existing Charaideo Civil Sub-Division of Sivasagar. SRC-193's 2011 administrative tables place Mahmora in that Charaideo subdivision. Current Charaideo official sources SRC-195 and SRC-205 list Mahmora under Charaideo; current Sivasagar profile SRC-221 no longer lists it.

This resolves an important chronology issue: **SRC-011's October 2015 sample date precedes the 25 January 2016 district-formation notification.** Therefore, the paper's use of “Sivasagar district” is not inconsistent with a sample collected in the Mahmora/Charaideo-subdivision territory. The earlier Mariani alternative remains inconsistent because Mariani was part of Jorhat district by 2015, and Kokilamukh also appears in the Jorhat 2011 DCHB (SRC-212).

## 3. A2 decision

Evidence chain:
1. SRC-011 records an Ahom Xajpani sample A2 at “Konwergaon”, collected October 2015.
2. SRC-193 records a village “Konwar Gaon”, code 293217, in Mahmora sub-district, then part of Sivasagar district's Charaideo Civil Sub-Division.
3. SRC-220 transfers the entire Charaideo Civil Sub-Division to the new Charaideo district in January 2016.
4. SRC-222 and SRC-223 independently confirm that “Mahmora Konwar Gaon” is a current Charaideo locality, with Bharali Pukhuri postal geography.

**Assessment: high-plausibility candidate; not a confirmed sample-site alias.** The spelling similarity (Konwergaon / Konwar Gaon), the exact Mahmora administrative unit, and the correct 2015 source-era district chronology make this the strongest A2 candidate. However, no source explicitly says that Handique et al.'s “Konwergaon” means Census village code 293217 or the current Mahmora Konwar Gaon. Keep ASM-GAP-076 open pending original sample register, author clarification, coordinates, or a formal historical alias/census-code crosswalk.

Do not add a Charaideo beverage-specific place row yet. The metal-profile article confirms a sample from the named source locality; until the alias is established, the sample cannot safely be attributed to the current named revenue village.

## 4. A1 “Bharalisuk” — exact-name search and false-friend control

Targeted searches for “Bharalisuk”, “Bharali Suk”, “Bharali Sukh”, and combinations with Sivasagar, Charaideo, Sonari and Mahmora did not recover an authoritative locality record matching the exact sample spelling.

The following nearby/current names are **not** alias proof:
- **Bharali Pukhuri** — current Charaideo GP/postal locality; SRC-179 lists the GP under Sonari (Mahmora).
- **Mahmora Konwar Gaon, P.O. Bharali Pukhuri** — SRC-223 gives a current address.
- **Bharali Pukhuri-A/B/C** — education clusters in official education datasets, not revenue villages.
- **Dulia Bharali Gaon** — a separate historical Teok/Jorhat locality (SRC-208/SRC-209).

A1 may be a transcription variant, a micro-locality, a household/local name, or a distinct village. None can be selected without the field register or author confirmation. Do not equate “Bharalisuk” with “Bharali Pukhuri” by shared initial wording.

## 5. Archive decision and next action

- Register SRC-222 (official Charaideo AAGY village-specific project page).
- Register SRC-223 (official 2025 CJM Charaideo admit card address).
- Update ASM-GAP-076: A2 upgraded to high-plausibility Mahmora candidate; clarify that October 2015 predates the January 2016 district notification; A1 remains unresolved.
- Keep the Charaideo place matrix unchanged; no beverage-specific place row.
- Next decisive retrieval: request Handique's October 2015 sample-site register, coordinates, and the full thesis pages 10–70 using Gauhati University registration no. 005263; alternatively seek written author clarification identifying the exact village and whether “Bharalisuk” is a local/spelling variant.

## 6. New competing A2 candidates — SRC-228 and SRC-229

### SRC-228: official 2021 Sivasagar election sector

The Government of Assam Personnel (A) Department notification No. AAP.54/2014/54, dated 6 March 2021, includes an election-administration schedule for Sivasagar. It lists **KONWERGAON** as **Zone 5, Sector 16**. This is a contemporary official Sivasagar-side occurrence of the name, and it prevents treating the Mahmora candidate as uniquely established. However, this is a sector deployment list, not a revenue-village register: it does not give a Census code, coordinates, a boundary map or a direct crosswalk. The sector may cover a settlement cluster.

### SRC-229: official 2011 Census PCA-TV catalogue

The Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner of India's official 2011 PCA-TV catalogue for undivided Sivasagar district (reference **PC11_PCA-TV-1812**) identifies several distinct village names that could be confused with the sample's “Konwergaon” spelling:

| 2011 village name | Census village code | 2011 sub-district code | Sub-district |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| No.2 Konwer Gaon | 292701 | 02071 | Sibsagar |
| 1 No. Konwar Gaon | 292961 | 02074 | Sonari |
| Konwar Gaon | 293217 | 02075 | Mahmora |
| No.1 Konwar | 292576 | 02070 | Dimow |
| Nahar Pukhuri Konwar Gaon | 293145 | 02074 | Sonari |

The first three are especially relevant exact/near-exact name candidates. The fourth and fifth are additional nearby-name false-positive controls. The official catalogue's geographic-unit listing explicitly identifies Mahmora as sub-district 02075 and lists village code 293217 under it; it separately identifies Sibsagar sub-district 02071 and No.2 Konwer Gaon code 292701, and Sonari sub-district 02074 and 1 No. Konwar Gaon code 292961.

### Revised A2 decision

Mahmora Konwar Gaon / code **293217** remains plausible because SRC-222/SRC-223 and the district-transfer order SRC-220 place a current locality of that name in Charaideo successor territory. But the Census catalogue now shows that the spelling is not unique within undivided Sivasagar. The 2021 election sector list adds another Sivasagar-side “KONWERGAON” lead, with no village-code mapping. Therefore A2 is **unresolved among multiple namesakes**; do not label Mahmora as the strongest/high-confidence match until sample coordinates, the original field register or an author confirmation narrows it.

No Charaideo beverage-specific place row is added. A1 Bharalisuk remains unresolved. The next decisive evidence is the 2015 sample-site register/coordinates; alternatively obtain the Sivasagar Election Office's Zone 5/Sector 16 map and compare its footprint with the Census village candidates and the 2016 Charaideo transfer boundary.


## 7. SRC-230 — Charaideo flood-plan locality: “No2 Konwar Gaon” under Sonari

**Official source:** District Disaster Management Authority, Charaideo, *Flood Contingency Plan 2024–25*, Government of Assam. URL: https://charaideo.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/public_utility/CONTINGENCY.pdf

The affected-village table explicitly records **“No2 Konwar Gaon”** and assigns it to **Sonari Revenue Circle** (SRC-230; PDF text extraction around page 4 / lines 135 in the indexed copy). This is a new official Charaideo-side locality lead and materially widens the A2 namesake problem: the sample spelling “Konwergaon” cannot be uniquely assigned to Mahmora merely because the latter is an established current Charaideo locality.

### Limits and non-equivalences

The flood plan is an emergency-management list, not a Census/revenue-village directory. It supplies no Census code, coordinates, village boundary, or statement that “No2 Konwar Gaon” is an alias of any 2011 Census entry. In particular, do **not** silently equate it with:

- No.2 Konwer Gaon — Census code 292701, Sibsagar sub-district 02071;
- 1 No. Konwar Gaon — Census code 292961, Sonari sub-district 02074;
- Konwar Gaon — Census code 293217, Mahmora sub-district 02075;
- No.2 Konwar — Census code 292558, Dimow sub-district 02070 (a separate entry).

The similar “No2” prefix is not sufficient to connect the flood-plan label to any of these. The exact revenue code and spatial footprint must be obtained from Sonari Revenue Circle or an official village map. Note also that the official 2011 catalogue lists **No.2 Konwar (292558)** under Dimow, not Sonari; this is another namesake and must not be confused with the flood-plan locality.

### Revised decision

A2 remains **unresolved among multiple namesakes**. Mahmora code 293217 is a plausible candidate, but not uniquely preferred or confirmed. The Sonari flood-plan listing establishes only that a current Charaideo administrative locality with a near-identical name exists. It does not identify the 2015 sample. Keep ASM-GAP-076 High/Open, do not add a beverage-specific Charaideo place row, and retain A1 “Bharalisuk” as unresolved.

**Next evidence request:** obtain the Sonari Revenue Circle village code/map for “No2 Konwar Gaon” and, above all, Handique's October 2015 sample-site register/coordinates or author confirmation of the exact A2 village.


## 8. Census village 292985 — strong Sonari continuity lead, but not yet the A2 sample match

### Official Census record (SRC-231)

The official Census of India 2011 PCA C.D.-block catalogue for undivided Sivasagar district lists **“No.2 Konwar” — village code 292985 — under sub-district code 02074 (Sonari)**. This must be distinguished from two other similarly named entries:

- **No.2 Konwer Gaon — 292701**, Sibsagar sub-district 02071;
- **No.2 Konwar — 292558**, Dimow sub-district 02070.

### Official current Charaideo record (SRC-230)

The Charaideo Flood Contingency Plan 2024–25 explicitly lists **“No2 Konwar Gaon” under Sonari Revenue Circle**. Matching the distinctive “No2 Konwar” name and Sonari administrative unit creates a strong continuity lead from the 2011 Census village 292985 to the current flood-plan locality. It is stronger than a shared surname or broad-district match, but it is still not a formal revenue-village code notification, coordinate match or cadastral alias statement.

### What this does — and does not — establish for A2

This is now an important candidate to test, but SRC-011 prints A2 simply as **“Konwergaon”**, without the “No.2” qualifier. No source currently establishes that the paper abbreviated the numbered village name, or that the field team meant this Sonari site rather than another Konwar/Konwer village. Do not silently add “No.2” to the published sample locality. A2 remains **unresolved**, with No.2 Konwar Gaon / 292985 now a strong administrative candidate for cross-checking against the sample register, alongside other namesakes. The original October 2015 field/sample register, coordinates or author confirmation remains decisive.

## 9. SRC-232 — Survey of India Charaideo administrative map

The 2025 Survey of India Assam Digital Data Register contains a Charaideo district map depicting the outer district boundary and internal **Mahmara, Sapekhati and Sonari** circle boundaries. It gives useful district/circle-scale geography for later georeferencing, but does not mark the disputed village or supply village coordinates. The Charaideo district profile and flood plan describe four revenue circles including Nazira part, whereas this Survey of India map depicts three; retain that source discrepancy rather than treating the map as a complete revenue-circle schedule.


## 10. SRC-233 — current Sivasagar Panchayat list adds numbered Konwar namesakes

**Official source:** Panchayat & Rural Development Department, Government of Assam, notification **PRD-11030/22/2024-PRD(A)**, *Gaon Panchayats and corresponding villages under Sivasagar District*. URL: https://pnrd.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/swf_utility_folder/departments/pnrd_uneecopscloud_com_oid_9/menu/document/sivasagar_gp.pdf

The notification says it partially modifies an earlier notification dated 15 October 2024 and publishes corresponding Gaon Panchayat/village lists under **current Sivasagar District** (SRC-233). In the Sivasagar section, the extracted table includes both **“No.2 Konwar”** and **“2 No. Konwar Gaon”** (PDF text around lines 682–752). This adds official present-day Sivasagar namesake controls to the already documented present-day Charaideo/Sonari “No2 Konwar Gaon” in SRC-230.

### Interpretation limits

The P&RD notification is a Panchayat/village association list, not a revenue-village code catalogue or cadastral map. It gives no Census codes or coordinates for these two labels. The table's extracted layout is grouped by Gaon Panchayat, but does not establish which 2011 Census code corresponds to either label. Do not assign them to Census entries 292558 (Dimow), 292701 (Sibsagar), or 292985 (Sonari) without an official code/geometry crosswalk.

The new source confirms that numbered Konwar namesake labels occur in the current Sivasagar administrative list as well as the current Charaideo/Sonari flood plan. It therefore **increases**, rather than reduces, the ambiguity of SRC-011 A2's unnumbered **“Konwergaon”**. It does not establish that A2 was any numbered village, nor that it was outside/inside current Charaideo.

### Updated decision

- A2 remains **unresolved**. Keep the sample's published spelling exactly as printed; do not add or remove a numbered prefix.
- No beverage-specific Charaideo place row.
- A1 “Bharalisuk” remains unresolved.
- Keep ASM-GAP-076 High/Open.

**Next decisive evidence:** obtain the official current Sivasagar GP-to-revenue-village code/geometry crosswalk and the Sonari Revenue Circle code/map for “No2 Konwar Gaon”; then compare those records with the October 2015 sample register/coordinates or obtain direct author confirmation of the sample locality.


## 11. SRC-234 — 2001-to-2011 Census location-code continuity for No.2 Konwar

**Official source:** Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India, *District Census Handbook — Sivasagar District, Part A (Village and Town Directory)*. URL: https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/240/download/610/DH_2011_1812_PART_A_DCHB_SIVASAGAR.pdf

The official 2011 DCHB's Lakwa CD Block village directory lists **No.2 Konwar**, 2011 Permanent Location Code Number (PLCN) **292985**, and its 2001 PLCN **01816600** (SRC-234). This is a useful historical continuity check: the 2011 Census entry is not merely a search-catalogue transcription; the district handbook itself prints the village name and both location codes.

### Boundary of the inference

This is a 2001-to-2011 Census code lineage, **not** a current revenue-village concordance. It does not provide coordinates or a village polygon, and it does not formally establish that the 2024–25 Charaideo flood-plan entry “No2 Konwar Gaon” under Sonari Revenue Circle (SRC-230) is the same cadastral unit. Even if that administrative continuity is later proven, the paper's A2 label remains the unnumbered **“Konwergaon”**; no evidence yet shows the authors omitted a “No.2” prefix.

### Precision note on SRC-233

The 2024 Assam P&RD notification is a schedule of Anchalik Panchayats, Gaon Panchayats and villages under **Sivasagar District**. In its Sivasagar section it contains several distinct similar labels, including **“No.2 Konwar”**, **“1 No. Konwar”**, **“2 No. Konwar Gaon”** and **“1 No. Konwargaon”** (SRC-233, PDF lines 682–786). These are separate printed entries in the schedule, not one interchangeable alias cluster. The notification does not provide Census codes or geometry for them. Their presence confirms that name-only matching is unsafe; it does not identify A2.

### Decision

A2 remains unresolved; A1 “Bharalisuk” remains unresolved. Keep ASM-GAP-076 High/Open and do not create a beverage-specific Charaideo place row. The next decisive retrieval is an official current Sonari Revenue Circle village-code/map crosswalk for “No2 Konwar Gaon”, plus the October 2015 sample-site register/coordinates or direct author confirmation.
