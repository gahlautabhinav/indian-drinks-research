## What this establishes

The three names are separate registered GI entries, each with its own application and certificate number. The registry’s displayed geographical-area field is “Assam.” This metadata should be transcribed as the registry’s field value, not interpreted as proof that production is uniformly distributed across every Assam district or village.

The registry pages list original Statement of Case PDFs, maps and producer lists. The accessible search/page output exposed the document names and dates but did not expose a usable PDF payload in this pass. Consequently, the following have **not** been extracted or verified here:
- product-specific definition and ingredient/substrate requirements;
- required or characteristic processing steps, including whether any distillation is specified for Jou Gwran;
- exact GI production-area boundary from the map/case statement;
- named producer list and any locality-level evidence it provides;
- the wording used to distinguish Jou Gishi from Maibra Jou Bidwi and broader published terms such as Jou bishi, Joubidwi, finai and Joufinai.

Authorised-user addresses displayed on the registry pages are leads for further verification, not a representative production map. They should not be converted into beverage-production place rows without corroboration.
