**Reviewed:** 2026-10-22  
**Scope:** Official Intellectual Property India public registry metadata for GI applications 968, 969 and 970. This is a registry/provenance audit, not a full extraction of the statements of case.

The registry pages report the applicant as Bodo Geographical Indication (G.I) Board, applicant address in Tengapara, Kokrajhar, BTR, Assam; filing date 29 August 2022; Class 32; goods classified as manufactured goods; geographical area listed as Assam; certificate date 1 October 2024; and validity through 28 August 2032.

These official records support the **registered product names and registration metadata**. They do not, by themselves, prove that a related spelling or a broader community term is synonymous with the registered product, nor do they establish current production in every Assam village.

## Authorized-user addresses: research leads only

The public registry pages display authorized-user addresses. The pages reviewed list these place names:

- **Deborbill / Amteka Bazar / Amguri** — appears in the authorized-user details for all three registrations.
- **West Dangarkutti / Dotoma / Dotma** — appears in the authorized-user details for all three registrations.
- **Batabari** — appears in the authorized-user details for all three registrations.
- **Palashbari / Polashbari** — appears in the reviewed details for application 970 (Bodo Maibra Jou Bidwi).

These are official **authorized-user address leads**, not verified production localities. The public page does not establish that the person currently produces the product at the listed address, that the place is the only production location, or that every registered product is produced at every shared address. Do not convert these addresses into confirmed village-level production claims without corroborating evidence.

## Statement-of-case extraction remains open

The official pages list downloadable documents named “Statement of Case” for each application, but the text of those PDFs has not yet been extracted in this pass. That remains the highest-value next step for the Bodo GI records:

1. Extract each statement of case and any official map/specification.
2. Record the exact definition, ingredients/substrate, preparation boundary and geographical-area wording for each registered product.
3. Compare those definitions with SRC-081 (Deka et al., 2025), which discusses Joubidwi, stored Joufinai and fruit-based Jougwran variants.
4. Update beverage records only where the registered specification supports a correction.
5. Keep authorized-user addresses and actual production observations in separate evidence categories.

## Repository interpretation

The existing beverage records already identify the official registrations and caution against treating applicant addresses as proof of universal production. This audit adds the observed authorized-user address leads while retaining that limitation. No canonical beverage record or village-level production claim is added from addresses alone.


## Document-level access check (2026-10-22)

The official registry pages expose a document inventory for each application, including a **Statement of Case dated 29 August 2022**, a **producers list dated 13 February 2023**, and subsequent amendments/annexures. The Statement of Case appears as item 24 for applications 968 and 969, and item 23 for application 970. The producers-list item appears as item 18 for application 968 and item 17 for applications 969 and 970.

The registry HTML makes the existence and labels of these documents verifiable, but the document links are rendered as generic PDF controls. In this research pass, the web retrieval layer did not return the PDF body for direct extraction. Therefore, this audit **does not claim to have read** the Statements of Case or producer lists, and it does not yet extract their product definitions, production steps, historical claims, village lists, or producer counts.

**Research rule:** do not substitute authorized-user addresses for a geographical production boundary. Do not use the state-level “Assam” field as proof that every district or village produces the registered beverage. The next meaningful advance is obtaining the actual Statement of Case and producers-list PDF contents, then recording document/page-level claims and keeping the registry metadata as a separate evidence layer.
