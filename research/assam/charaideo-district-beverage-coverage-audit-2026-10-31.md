# Charaideo district beverage-coverage audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-31  
**District:** Charaideo, Assam  
**Outcome:** No new beverage-specific place record accepted; district remains an evidence gap.

## Administrative context

The Government of Assam district page states Charaideo was carved out of Sivasagar district under a 2016 government notification, with Sonari as district headquarters. This matters because older references to Sivasagar cannot automatically be assigned to current Charaideo; source date and exact locality are required. Official district page: https://charaideo.assam.gov.in/about-us/about-district

## Beverage evidence reviewed

### 1. Ahom Xajpani in Sivasagar — not transferable to Charaideo

Existing registered studies SRC-011 (Handique et al., 2017), SRC-021 (Das, Deka & Miyaji, 2012), and SRC-026 (Duarah et al., 2023) describe Ahom Xajpani / Xaj / Haaj / Laopani and starter-culture terminology in Sivasagar district. Their available geographic descriptions do not name a village in current Charaideo. The 2017 analytical paper explicitly names Sivasagar district, but its methods date sample collection to October 2015 (before the district notification cited in official Charaideo history as 2016). The article's Table 1 names two Ahom sample sites, A1 Bharalisuk and A2 Konwergaon. Thus it is no longer accurate to dismiss the paper simply as a post-creation Sivasagar study; it is a potentially relevant historical-locality lead whose two spellings still need a reliable present-day boundary crosswalk.

### 2. Demou commercial beverage report — Sivasagar, not Charaideo

SRC-117 (Open Magazine, 2024) reports a commercial Luk Lao production unit at Demou village, Sivasagar district. The article's product identity and relationship to Xajpani/Laopani remain unresolved. It does not provide evidence for Charaideo. Do not relocate Demou to Charaideo or equate Luk Lao with other Ahom beverage names.

### 3. General Ahom / Lao-Pani material — insufficient locality attribution

Search results included general Ahom Xajpani descriptions and a non-authoritative travel page that mentions home-brewed Lao-Pani in Charaideo. The latter does not provide a traceable producer interview, named village/household, primary source or verifiable methodology; it is not accepted as canonical geographic evidence. General community traditions cannot establish district-level production.

## Decision

- Keep Charaideo at zero beverage-specific place rows.
- Do not transfer Sivasagar district-level Xajpani evidence into Charaideo based on proximity, Ahom historical association or the former district boundary.
- Keep Demou/Luk Lao under its existing source-attributed Sivasagar record; do not reassign or merge the product identity.
- This is an evidence gap, not evidence that Charaideo lacks beverage traditions.

## Next research route

Seek named Sonari/Charaideo-area Ahom household or community sources, local cultural institution records, and direct producer evidence. Any older Sivasagar source should be mapped using the precise named village and 2016 boundary crosswalk before inclusion.


## 2026-11-13 follow-up — SRC-011 named Ahom Xajpani sample sites

A full-text recheck of Handique (2017; SRC-011) materially improves the locality evidence. The methods state that six villages densely populated by Ahom, Mising and Deori communities in Sivasagar district were sampled in **October 2015**. The article labels two Ahom samples A1 and A2; Table 1 gives their collection sites as **Bharalisuk** (A1) and **Konwergaon** (A2). The paper's footnote explicitly defines A as Ahom. This is direct sample-locality evidence for Ahom rice beer/Xajpani at those source-reported localities, but it does not by itself prove that either spelling maps to a current Charaideo revenue village.

The administrative chronology needs careful wording. The 2024 Charaideo District Survey Report says Charaideo was declared a new district on 15 August 2015 and carved out of Sivasagar; the district administration history cites Government notification No. GAG(B) 27/01/2016. SRC-011's samples were collected in October 2015 and the article retains the Sivasagar label. The archive should preserve the source-era district wording rather than silently relabel the samples as Charaideo.

Two administrative crosswalk leads were checked:
- The 2021 Assam PHE government annexure lists **Bharali Pukhuri** as a gaon panchayat under Sonari (Mahmora), Charaideo. Similarity to the article's **Bharalisuk** is not enough to equate the sample locality with that panchayat or a village inside it.
- Current directory results show multiple similarly named **Konwar Gaon / No. 2 Konwar** records in Charaideo. The article supplies no census village code, circle, panchayat, coordinates or spelling variant that uniquely identifies which one, if any, is its **Konwergaon**.

**Decision:** create no Charaideo place row and do not move either sample to Charaideo. Register SRC-178 (Charaideo District Survey Report) and SRC-179 (Assam PHE administrative annexure); create ASM-GAP-076 for the exact locality-to-current-boundary crosswalk and author/field-register retrieval. Keep Charaideo at zero verified beverage-specific place rows until both sites are uniquely resolved. The new finding is a stronger lead, not a negative finding and not proof of current household prevalence.

Next: obtain historical 1981-to-2011 village-code concordances and the October 2015 author sample register; do not promote either substring/name match.

## 2026-11-14 — 1981–2011 Teok continuity check for Dulia Bharali Gaon (SRC-209)

Cross-checked SRC-208's 1981 Sibsagar Village Directory against the official 2011 Jorhat PCA TV dataset and Jorhat District Census Handbook Part B. The 1981 directory lists **Dulia Bharali Gaon** under Teok Police Station, location code **03/6/3/82** (printed p.67). The 2011 Jorhat PCA TV directory lists **Dulia Bharali Gaon**, village code **293808**, under Teok sub-district code **02079**; the 2011 Jorhat DCHB Part B also lists the village and code. This is a strong name-and-administrative-unit continuity lead from the 1981 Teok PS entry to the 2011 Teok sub-district village.

Important limit: this is not a formal location-code concordance, and neither census directory calls the village “Bharalisuk.” It does **not** establish that SRC-011's October 2015 A1 sample came from Dulia Bharali Gaon. It only prevents the 1981/2011 Dulia Bharali Gaon record from being confused with the beverage sample locality. No A1 assignment to Jorhat or Charaideo is justified without the original author field register.

**Decision unchanged:** A1 Bharalisuk remains unresolved. A2 Mahmora Konwar Gaon / 2011 Sivasagar code 293217 remains a strong candidate only; no beverage-specific place row is added.

## 2026-11-14 — A2 Mariani alternative and 2015 district-label inconsistency (SRC-210)

A fresh historical cross-check reveals a second plausible “Konwergaon” candidate. The official 1981 Sibsagar Village & Town Directory lists **Konwar Gaon** under **Mariani Police Station**, location code **03/6/4/59** (SRC-208; printed p.95). The official 2001 Jorhat Village Directory lists **Konwar Gaon**, code **01904800**, under Mariani tehsil (SRC-210). The official 2011 Jorhat PCA TV directory lists **Kowar Gaon**, code **294090**, under Mariani sub-district code **02081** (SRC-209). Name plus administrative continuity makes these a plausible 1981→2001→2011 chain, with an orthographic shift Konwar→Kowar; however, no formal code-concordance/alias document has been located.

This candidate creates a material source-level geography issue: the 2017 paper says its six village samples were collected in October 2015 in **Sivasagar district** (SRC-011), but Mariani had been part of Jorhat district since Jorhat was carved out of undivided Sibsagar in 1983. Thus, if A2 were the Mariani Konwar/Kowar Gaon, the paper's district label would be geographically inaccurate or used loosely/historically. This cannot be assumed. The alternative 2011 Sivasagar candidate **Konwar Gaon, code 293217, Mahmora sub-district** remains plausible as well, but is also unconfirmed.

**Decision:** retain both A2 candidates as alternatives only: (1) Mahmora Konwar Gaon, Sivasagar code 293217; (2) Mariani Konwar/Kowar Gaon, 1981 code 03/6/4/59 → 2001 code 01904800 → 2011 code 294090, Jorhat. Neither is assigned to the 2015 sample. A1 Bharalisuk remains unresolved. No beverage-specific place row is added. The next decisive evidence is the October 2015 sample-site register/coordinates and author clarification of the “Sivasagar district” wording.

Sources: SRC-208, SRC-209, SRC-210; official Jorhat district history (Jorhat carved from undivided Sibsagar in 1983): https://jorhat.assam.gov.in/about-us/history-jorhat; SRC-011: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jib.413

### Administrative chronology control — SRC-211

The official Jorhat district history (SRC-211) confirms Jorhat was a subdivision of undivided Sibsagar and was carved out as a separate district in 1983. This matters for the Mariani candidate: a 1981 Mariani PS entry can legitimately appear in the Sibsagar District Census Handbook, but a Mariani locality in October 2015 would fall under Jorhat's post-1983 district administration. Therefore the 2017 article's statement that all six samples were collected in Sivasagar district cannot be reconciled with the Mariani A2 candidate without author clarification or the original field register. The source may have a district-label error, the Mariani name may be a false friend, or A2 may refer to another Konwar Gaon such as the Mahmora candidate; none can be selected from the present evidence.
