The district matrix now cross-references SRC-061 as study-area evidence for all six named districts: Baksa, Biswanath, Golaghat, Lakhimpur, Sivasagar, and Sonitpur. ### Full-text follow-up — SRC-096 to SRC-098

**SRC-096 (Tanti et al. 2010):** the full-text PDF was available through the NIScPR repository. The methods report 440 participants, of whom 242 supplied information across all methods; prior informed consent is reported. The paper documents starter/beverage pairs including Adivasi Dabai/Haria, Dimasa Hamao/Zu, Ahom Xajar Pitha/Xajpani, Mising Apong kusure/Apong, Bodo emao/Zu and Karbi Thap/Arak. It also describes Nagin-Apong and Poro-Apong as differing in substrate/starter handling. The comparative table was transcribed in [the SRC-096 extraction note](src-096-table-extraction.md). It gives community/state and starter/beverage pairings but no verifiable village-by-community mapping for the Assam entries, so no village place rows were added; historical community labels and plant identifications require care.

**SRC-098 (Chakrabarty et al. 2014):** full-text PDF confirms a selected-product study involving Judima and the Humao starter, with observation/interviews and microbial/nutritional characterization. This does not establish a district-wide census or village-level distribution.

The Judima dossier now cites SRC-097 and SRC-098 as historical district-level evidence. No new place row or beverage identity merge was created.
