02 / RESEARCH METHOD

Read the evidence
in its context.

This is a working research archive, not a definitive catalogue of every community practice. Each claim should remain traceable to its source and geographic scope.

01
Terminology

Names and spellings are retained with source context. Similar names are not assumed to be synonyms.

02
Geography

A sample, historical mention or community-level study does not establish current production in every locality.

03
Uncertainty

Conflicting accounts, missing locality evidence and unanswered questions stay visible in the record.
