HOW TO READ THIS ARCHIVE

Evidence, not assumptions.

Traditional knowledge is diverse and local. This archive separates what a source documents from what still needs confirmation.

01Names stay contextual

Spelling variants are not automatically treated as separate drinks—or as perfect synonyms.

02Place matters

A research sample or historical mention is not proof of current production across a whole district.

03Gaps are part of the record

Unresolved terminology, source limitations and missing village-level evidence stay visible.
